# The Adversarial Ledger

*What an opposing physicist will actually say — ranked by the damage it does — and what closes each attack. Written honestly, because the framework's only defense is that its holes are named, sized, and scheduled. The honest answer to "can we dismantle any objection": no — not yet. But every attack below has a known closing move, and two of them are executable against data that already exists.*

---

## The attacks, ranked

### 1. "Your closures are definitional, not predictions."

**The attack:** \(M_{\rm parent}\) is derived from the same \(H_0, \Omega_\Lambda\) that define the targets; \(C = 1\) is exact algebra; the consistency audit's Part B largely verifies ΛCDM's self-consistency — something Planck already did. The "identities" hold because they were built to hold.

**Force:** **High.** The one-number chain, the temperature ratio, the entropy equality — all are exact consequences of the definitions plus the saturation assumption. They are *coherence proofs*, not independent measurements.

**Closing move (executable now):** recompute every closure with **independent datasets in place of Planck's combination** — DESI BAO's Ω_Λ, Pantheon+ SN distances, eBOSS/BAO H₀ — each identity must hold with *any* independent input pair, not just the one it was derived with. A closure that survives dataset substitution is a prediction; one that only works with its own inputs is bookkeeping.

**Status: EXECUTED.** All five DESI DR2 datasets (BAO-only, +BBN, +θ*, +CMB, FS+BAO) hold every closure at 0.75–2.0σ — the known dataset-tension level, no more. See `the-consistency-audit.md` §2b. Attack 1 is answered.

### 2. "The spectral tilt has a free parameter."

**The attack:** \(n_s = 1 - 12.4(\sigma/\theta)^2\) with \(\sigma/\theta = 5.3\%\) was *tuned* to the data. Until the shear at crossing is derived from the parent's Kerr spin, this is a mechanism, not a prediction. The crossing-time formula is semi-analytic; the amplitude \(A_s\) is entirely open; the full two-region junction is unconstructed.

**Force:** **High — the biggest remaining hole.**

**Closing moves:**
- **(EXECUTED — see `the-consistency-audit.md` §3b.)** The direction-dependent tilt: a quadrupolar variation of the *spectral index itself* was pre-registered, then measured in the real Planck SMICA maps (2 GB downloaded, healpy pipeline, 552 patches, rotation test). Result: **NOT CONFIRMED** — measured ratio 0.15 vs predicted [0.25, 0.81]; the marginal (~2σ) quadrupole is consistent with noise/residual systematics; the raw-sky quadrupole was shown to track the galactic mask (identified and mitigated). Honest classification: unconfirmed at current sensitivity; LiteBIRD/CMB-S4 is the referee. The pre-registration held — the test was executable, the verdict defensible, the negative recorded as designed.
- **(Executable now.)** The full PPU-style perturbation derivation of the crossing-time formula, so the coefficient 12.4 is derived, not fitted.
- **(Open.)** The junction end-to-end: dust outer region glued to the KS throat, modes extracted in the child frame, \(A_s\) from the same computation, \(\sigma/\theta\) from the Kerr \(a_*\).

### 3. "The junction does not exist."

**The attack:** the strict KS+spin solution has a *past pancake singularity* — contradicting the framework's own no-infinities theorem unless the outer region genuinely replaces it. The two-region gluing was never completed; the child-frame mode extraction was never demonstrated. Until then the model is internally incomplete.

**Force:** **High — the deepest internal gap.**

**Closing move:** the junction computation, end to end, with the singularity replaced (not patched) — the honest deliverable that turns the pancake into the parent's dust history.

### 4. "Your best observational evidence is a posteriori."

**The attack:** the CMB anomalies are 2–2.5σ each; the corpus itself retracted the 6.5σ headline for post-hoc selection. "You predicted the anomalies after seeing them."

**Force:** **Medium-high.** The PR4 persistence is genuine but does not convert retrodiction into prediction.

**Closing moves:** LiteBIRD/CMB-S4 polarimetry (the referees, scheduled) — and, now: the direction-dependent-tilt test (§2), which is a *pre-registered* prediction testable against existing Planck maps.

### 5. "The model is indistinguishable from ΛCDM today."

**The attack:** every closure reproduces ΛCDM by construction; every discriminator is future-dated (DESI/Euclid w, LiteBIRD, FCC) except the anomalies (a posteriori) and the collider nulls (shared with many models).

**Force:** **Medium** — until §2's tilt test or §1's dataset-substitution is executed.

### 6. "Assumptions wearing derivations' clothing."

The saturation boundary condition, the Ω_Λ self-similarity across levels, the torsion filter, the classical Weyssenhoff fluid — each asserted, each in need of a derivation or a sharper test.

**Force:** **Medium.** Each has an instrumented referee (w-gate; the chain recursion's fixed-point argument; HL-LHC/FCC; the bounce numerics).

### 7. "Unfinished stated claims."

P1 failed as computed (the MESA-class scan is the remaining door); P4's "1D/3D" promise has only the 1D/RK4 verification.

**Force:** **Low-medium** — admitted in the corpus, scheduled.

## The honest verdict

The framework currently holds: a **coherence proof** (instruments consistent with each other at 15 digits), a **possibility proof** (the observation ledger), **retrodictions** (the anomaly pattern, the null collider results, the warm band), and a **certified pipeline** (de Sitter benchmark). It does not yet hold: a **forward prediction tested against present data**, an **independent-input closure check**, or a **constructed junction**.

The path from here to "dismantle any objection" is exactly two computations, both executable against data that already exists: (1) the quadrupolar spectral-index test on Planck full-sky maps, and (2) the dataset-substitution audit. Everything else is scheduled.
