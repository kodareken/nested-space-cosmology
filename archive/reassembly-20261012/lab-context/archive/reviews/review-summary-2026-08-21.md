scientific discipline. Dark matter and dark energy remain names for strongly

## What was corrected

### Causal speed and light

`c` was not created by stars or photons. It is the local causal-cone invariant of relativistic field equations. The hot early universe contained photons before stars. Infinite space does not imply infinite `c`. A Schwarzschild-coordinate velocity tending to zero is not a local measurement of a slower photon.

### Entropy and the cosmological constant

Unitarity does not imply equality of parent black-hole and child de Sitter coarse-grained entropies. Their equality is now an explicit saturation postulate. Solving the resulting formula using observed `Lambda` infers a parent scale; substituting it back does not predict `Lambda`.

### Bounce dynamics

ECKS torsion is algebraic and spin sourced in the minimal theory, but a bounce depends on the matter model, correlations/averaging, shear, and particle production. LQC bounce results apply to specified symmetry-reduced quantum models. Neither universally proves an astrophysical black-hole-to-child transition.

### CMB and DESI

The local Planck maps are PR3; literature statements about NPIPE/PR4 are distinct. A direct 2025 PR4 regional analysis found fitted `n_s` consistent with isotropy. DESI arXiv:2503.14738 is a DR2 BAO cosmology paper, not a DR2 full-shape analysis. Its dynamical-dark-energy preference is dataset/model dependent and is neither a confirmation nor a decisive falsification of the saturation branch.

### Particle and spin extensions

The claimed torsion filter, guaranteed absence of squarks, inherited neutralino dark matter, quantitative baryogenesis, and derivation of all galactic spin were unsupported. They are removed from the minimal model rather than defended through analogy.
