1. Horizons: Causal Boundaries Standard GR

A causal boundary forms when the "flow" of spacetime geometry matches the local causal speed \(c\). This unifies black-hole horizons and cosmological horizons under a single principle.

Schwarzschild Horizon

For a non-rotating black hole of mass \(M\), the horizon sits at:

\[ r_s = \frac{2GM}{c^2} \]

The average density inside this radius scales as \(M^{-2}\):

\[ \rho_{\rm avg} = \frac{3c^6}{32\pi G^3 M^2} \]

⟶ Larger black holes can have lower average density than water — but this is a coordinate bookkeeping quantity, not the physical interior density.

Cosmological Horizon

In an expanding universe, the Hubble radius is:

\[ R_H = \frac{c}{H} \]

The mass enclosed at critical density \(\rho_c = 3H^2/(8\pi G)\) is \(M_H \approx c^3/(2GH)\), and its Schwarzschild radius equals \(R_H\):

\[ r_s(M_H) = \frac{c}{H} = R_H \]

⟶ This compactness coincidence (\(C=1\)) is the central numerical fact linking black-hole and cosmological horizons.

Geometry Flow Unification

In the river model, outgoing light near a black hole satisfies:

\[ \frac{dr}{dt}_{\rm out} = c - \sqrt{\frac{2GM}{r}} \]

For an expanding universe, incoming light satisfies:

\[ \dot{D} = H D - c \]

Both hit zero when geometry-flow equals causal speed. This is a structural analogy — not an identity — but it unifies the concept of a horizon as a causal membrane.

2. Horizon Thermodynamics Standard GR + QFT

Horizons are not just geometric surfaces — they carry entropy and radiate with a temperature. This is one of the deepest results of theoretical physics.

Bekenstein–Hawking Entropy

\[ S_{\rm BH} = \frac{k_B A}{4\ell_P^2}, \quad \ell_P = \sqrt{\frac{\hbar G}{c^3}} \]

For a spherical horizon \(A = 4\pi r_s^2\), so \(S_{\rm BH} \propto M^2\) — more mass, exponentially more hidden information.

Hawking Temperature

\[ T_H = \frac{\hbar c^3}{8\pi G M k_B} \]

Large black holes are extremely cold. A solar-mass black hole has \(T_H \sim 10^{-7}\) K.

Gibbons–Hawking Temperature (de Sitter)

Cosmological horizons also radiate:

\[ T_{\rm GH} = \frac{\hbar H}{2\pi k_B}, \quad R_{\rm dS} = \sqrt{\frac{3}{\Lambda}} \]

Information Constraint

Black-hole entropy bounds the information content of everything behind the horizon. If a black hole spawns a child universe and emits Hawking radiation:

\[ S_{\rm rad} + S_{\rm child} - I({\rm rad}:{\rm child}) \le S_H \]

Information cannot be duplicated — the Page curve and modern replica-wormhole calculations strongly suggest Hawking radiation is unitary. Any baby-universe scenario must respect this constraint.

3. Bounce Cosmology Loop Quantum Gravity

Classical GR predicts singularities — infinite density at the Big Bang and inside black holes. Quantum gravity replaces these with bounces at finite density.

Loop Quantum Cosmology Bounce

When density \(\rho\) approaches the critical Planck-scale density \(\rho_c \sim 5 \times 10^{96} \;\rm kg/m^3\), quantum geometry generates effective repulsion:

\[ H^2 = \frac{8\pi G}{3}\rho\left(1 - \frac{\rho}{\rho_c}\right) \]

At \(\rho = \rho_c\): \(H^2 \to 0\) — expansion halts, then reverses. Collapse → Stillness → Expansion.

The scale factor \(a(t)\) (blue) bounces at Planck density; density (orange, dashed) peaks at \(\rho_c\).

From Bounce to Inflation

After the bounce, quantum corrections vanish and classical expansion takes over. If driven by an inflationary scalar field, the universe expands by \(e^N\) with \(N \gtrsim 60\), stretching a subatomic bounce region to cosmic scales and smoothing out curvature.

⟶ Note: LQC bounce results are best-established for symmetric cosmological models. Extension to realistic black-hole interiors is an open research problem.

4. Parent → Child Transition Conjecture

The hardest physical question: what exactly happens when a black-hole interior bounces into a new universe, and how do the rules change across the transition?

The Transition Kernel Open

A complete theory would need a quantum channel mapping collapse states to child-universe states + Hawking radiation:

\[ V_X : \mathcal{H}_{\rm collapse}(X,\theta_n) \to \mathcal{H}_{\rm Hawking}(X,\theta_n) \otimes \mathcal{H}_{\rm child}(X,\theta_{n+1}) \]

With unitarity \(V_X^\dagger V_X = \mathbb{I}\) and the child state obtained by tracing out radiation:

\[ \rho_{\rm child} = \operatorname{Tr}_{\rm Hawking}[V_X \rho_{\rm collapse} V_X^\dagger] \]

⟶ No quantum gravity theory currently specifies \(V_X\). This is a requirements document for what a complete theory would need to supply.

Mutation of Constants Conjecture

Dimensionless parameters (fine-structure constant \(\alpha\), mass ratios \(m_e/m_p\)) may drift slightly between generations:

\[ \theta_{n+1} = \theta_n + \frac{1}{\sqrt{S_H/k_B}} \, A(X) \, \xi \]

Larger parent horizons → smaller mutation steps. This follows Smolin's cosmological natural selection: universes that produce more black holes have more descendants, and the ensemble is biased towards parameters that maximise black-hole formation.

Entropy Budget

The child universe's Hilbert space dimension is bounded by the entropy of the parent black hole. Information cannot be created — only redistributed between the Hawking radiation output and the child universe interior.

5. Dark Energy as Horizon Memory Conjecture

If a child universe inherits its information budget from the parent black-hole horizon, then the cosmological constant \(\Lambda\) is not fundamental — it is a memory of the parent's size.

The Derivation

Equate parent black-hole entropy to child de Sitter entropy:

\[ S_{\rm BH} = \frac{k_B \pi r_s^2}{\ell_P^2} = \frac{k_B \cdot 3\pi}{\Lambda \ell_P^2} = S_{\rm dS} \]

Cancelling common factors yields a direct link between \(\Lambda\) and the parent black-hole radius:

\[ \Lambda = \frac{3}{r_s^2} = \frac{3c^4}{4G^2 M_{\rm parent}^2} \]

For a parent black hole of mass \(\sim 5 \times 10^{22} M_\odot\), this gives \(\Lambda \sim 10^{-52} \; \rm m^{-2}\) — remarkably close to the observed value.

A gargantuan parent horizon → a tiny cosmological constant in the child. Dark energy as horizon memory.

⟶ This is a conjecture, not a derived result. The derivation assumes (without proof) that \(S_{\rm BH} = S_{\rm dS}\) — there is no established principle requiring the two entropies to match. Treat this as a hypothesis awaiting a microphysical mechanism.

6. The Eternal Engine

The full picture: infinity emerges not from unbounded spatial extent, but from an endless process of compression → transition → expansion → structure formation → renewed collapse.

The Key Insight

\(\Lambda\)CDM cosmology emerges as the effective interior theory inside each pocket. The recursive horizon physics only operates at the boundary — the origin (bounce) and the asymptotic future (dark energy as horizon memory). Inside each pocket, standard cosmology works exactly as observed.
