## Rules and stop interpretation

The criterion remains `8 * U12**2 <= L01**2` (`p >= 3/2`), including the
inherited exact-zero and enclosure-debit-only cases. Corrected reconstruction
does not confer a resolved order on the actual binary64 path. Its public fine
debit is the corrected finest-pair upper bound plus the complete fine-path
accumulation bound. The large-base control preserves raw `D01=1/36`,
`D12=1/72`, corrected zero differences, and a nonzero `1/4` debit.

RHS evaluation error, nonlinear stability, global PDE error, source and
constraint health, spatial convergence, and cross-method agreement remain
independent obligations. The embedded estimator likewise supplies conditional
finite-data evidence, not a global solution-error theorem.

Localizer stops may leave a classified prefix and continue to the next
predeclared group. A global resource stop forbids subsequent work. Resource
receipts are never converted to scientific nonpasses; an unresolved channel
cannot establish a counterexample. A raw wall-clock interruption is
authenticated without inventing an identical timing replay. No such global
resource interruption occurred in this diagnostic.
