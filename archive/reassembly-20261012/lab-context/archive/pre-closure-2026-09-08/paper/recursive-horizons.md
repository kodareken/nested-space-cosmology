## Abstract

We investigate whether a collapsing black-hole interior can pass through a finite transition into an expanding cosmological domain while retaining the established local content of general relativity, quantum field theory, thermodynamics, and ΛCDM. The central synthesis is that a causal domain may be a stable phase: its geometry, vacuum state, matter excitations, transport, and information budget may be different limits of one covariant system. Collapse and expansion could then be opposite branches of one process rather than rival descriptions. This proposition joins established ingredients in a new global relation; it is not already implied by them.

We call the guiding thermodynamic conjecture **Finite Gradient Closure
(FGC)**. It distinguishes a completed infinite state from unbounded
continuation. A singular endpoint and an actually completed infinite physical
domain are treated as explanatory failures; an unending sequence of finite
nested gradients need not be. “Multiverse” here therefore means gradients
within gradients: every realized domain and transition must carry finite state
data, possess a lower closure and an upper closure or finite handoff, and
satisfy local conservation and the appropriate entropy law, even if the
generative sequence has no final step. This is a selection principle to be
implemented and tested, not a proof that mathematical infinity or every
standard asymptotic construction is unphysical, and not a license for
perpetual work extraction from equilibrium.

A divergent invariant, geodesic incompleteness, or failed extension may mark
the boundary of a retained description, but it does not encode what lies past
that boundary. An infinite decimal expansion likewise represents one number;
its repeated digits are not a catalogue of successor spacetimes. Evidence for
recursive continuation requires additional covariant dynamics that maps finite
predecessor state through a finite transition to finite successor state while
closing constraints, flux, entropy, and causal orientation. The map must then
be shown composable. A singular GR control is therefore a baseline, not a
positive transition result.

The motivating intuition is gradient based: observable states require differences, fluxes respond to gradients, and stable regimes can arise from competing terms. DSF-1 translates “constants as domain settings” into an operational hypothesis about dimensionless couplings, mass ratios, thermal ratios, and relative characteristic cones. It does not assert that mathematical constants vary, that a unit-dependent value of `c` is observable across domains, or that local light speed has already been shown to change. A conformal metric change leaves the null cone invariant; a physical speed claim requires a nonconformal characteristic structure and a dimensionless comparison such as `c_mode/c_gamma`.

The subordinate Nested Gradient Spectrum hypothesis uses literal QFT and
statistical-physics language: modes, spectral density, bandwidth, occupation,
resolution, and coarse-graining. It asks whether finite localization opens a
richer but still finite accessible spectrum while coarser enclosing levels
impose boundary and capacity constraints. Unresolved levels may be tested as
effective dark sources, but no dark-sector identification follows from the
idea. A universal “one hertz per black hole,” perfect equilibrium, or golden
ratio is not assumed; each requires an observer-independent spectral or
recurrence derivation.

The methodological posture is **we see things for what they truly are:
open-minded without madness**. Dark matter and dark energy retain their
observational burdens without being promoted into uniquely established
microscopic substances. A gradient or relational replacement receives the

## Plain-language summary

The image behind this project is simple. A black hole is not merely a dark ball in space; it is a causal region whose interior geometry evolves differently from its exterior. General relativity describes that interior as a collapsing anisotropic spacetime. Quantum-gravity models sometimes replace singular collapse with a bounce. Horizon thermodynamics assigns finite entropy to black-hole and cosmological horizons. Those facts invite a question: could collapse, a finite transition, and expansion be consecutive phases of one causal process?

Version 0.7 sharpens the proposed answer by restricting what “one globally connected solution” must mean. A “domain” is not a sphere drawn inside another sphere. It is a regime in which geometry, causal cones, vacuum stress, field excitations, and transport settle into a reproducible relationship. A local core, a parent metric, and a drawn shell do not become one solution until the matching, quasi-local flux, causal structure, and stability equations are solved. The attraction of this picture is not that it discards Einstein, Hawking, thermodynamics, quantum fields, or ΛCDM. It asks whether their successful equations are the local limits of one larger process.

That idea already has calculable pieces. A positive scalar-potential offset behaves like vacuum energy; rapid quadratic scalar oscillations behave like pressureless matter on average; a balanced source term can transfer their energy into radiation; and positive-`Lambda` geometry can oppose compact-mass attraction at a definite radius. The repository now computes these facts. Their union is a research direction, not yet an identification of the cosmic dark sector or a proof of ancestry.

That question is reasonable. Its answer is not already contained in the ingredients. A coordinate change showing that the Schwarzschild interior resembles a cosmology does not create a child universe. A bounce in a symmetric model does not establish a realistic black-hole bounce. Unitarity does not equate two coarse-grained horizon entropies. A numerical equality produced by solving for an unknown parent mass from the observed cosmological constant is not a prediction of that constant.

The useful move is to keep the question and place certainty exactly where the equations earn it. We can then ask what field selects the effective geometry and dimensionless settings, what balance stabilizes a domain, what changes that balance, how null rays pass from trapped to expanding regions, how the hot plasma and clustered matter arise, how information crosses the transition, and what observation would differ from standard cosmology. That is a grand proposal in a form a physicist can calculate, criticize, or falsify.

# 1. The epistemic contract

The purpose of this work is to match reality, not to win an argument. We therefore use six categories throughout:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Established** | A result of a mature theory or observation, restricted to its demonstrated domain |
| **Observed** | A published constraint with a named dataset, release, model, estimator, and uncertainty |
| **Derived** | An identity or conditional consequence of explicitly stated premises |
| **Hypothesized** | A new physical postulate introduced here |
| **Analogy** | A reasoning aid that does not itself supply gravitational dynamics |
| **Open / negative / retired** | A missing derivation, failed test, invalid method, or abandoned branch |

Three distinctions matter especially.

First, an **identity** is not an independent prediction. If `x` is defined from observed `y` using `y=f(x)`, then computing `f(x)` returns `y` by construction.

Second, a **coordinate statement** is not necessarily a local measurement. Schwarzschild-coordinate `dr/dt` can approach zero at a horizon while a freely falling observer measures nearby light at the invariant speed `c`.

Third, a result established in a **symmetry-reduced model** is not automatically generic. A homogeneous isotropic LQC bounce or a spin-fluid ECKS bounce demonstrates possibility under stated assumptions; it does not prove the outcome of rotating, inhomogeneous stellar collapse.

This contract does not make the project timid. It makes the ambitious part visible.

# 2. Why gradients are a productive starting point

## 2.1 Difference makes dynamics observable

The project’s central intuition is that a perfectly featureless state cannot define observable change. Physics supports a precise, limited version of that claim.

In quantum mechanics the Hamiltonian generates evolution [27],

```text
i hbar partial_t |psi> = H |psi>,
```

and observable phase evolution between stationary states depends on an energy difference,

```text
omega_nm = (E_n - E_m) / hbar.
```

An overall shift of all energies is normally unobservable in nongravitational quantum mechanics; differences generate relative phase. In linear nonequilibrium thermodynamics, gradients of thermodynamic potentials act as generalized forces that drive corresponding fluxes [29]. In field theory, gradients contribute kinetic energy and propagation.

The scope matters. “Energy generates time” is not a universal global theorem in arbitrary curved spacetime. A conserved energy arises from time-translation symmetry [28]; generic expanding FLRW spacetime has no global timelike Killing vector and no unique globally conserved total energy. Local stress-energy conservation, `nabla_mu T^{mu nu}=0`, remains the appropriate statement.

The programme can therefore begin with a field or order parameter `Phi`, its gradients, and a dynamical law. It cannot begin and end with the word “gradient.”

## 2.2 From gradient language to a model

A minimal effective description would take a form such as

```text
S = integral d^4x sqrt(-g) [ R/(2 kappa)
                            + L_Phi(Phi, nabla Phi)
                            + L_matter
                            + L_interaction ].
```

If geometry is emergent rather than fundamental, the model must instead define an effective metric,

```text
g_eff(mu,nu) = G_mu,nu[Phi, nabla Phi, state, ...],
```

and demonstrate that perturbations obey hyperbolic equations with a stable Lorentzian signature. Analogue-gravity systems show that perturbations in a medium can experience effective horizons and metrics [16,17]. Jacobson showed that Einstein’s equation can be read as an equation of state under specific local horizon assumptions [4]. These are legitimate precedents for asking whether geometry is collective. They do not establish this particular substrate.

## 2.3 Finite Gradient Closure as a fail-capable law

FGC makes the gradient intuition conditional and testable. When gravitational
localization steepens past a dynamically derived invariant threshold, a
healthy higher-order geometric or matter response must acquire the restoring
sign in the physical collapse channel and grow faster than the lower-order
focusing term. It must dominate at a nonzero finite scale before geodesic
incompleteness or effective-theory breakdown, while preserving constraints,
causal propagation, flux, and generalized entropy. No matter sector or
black-hole genealogy is selected by this statement alone.

Two toy controls expose the mathematical burden. A spatial mode energy

```text
E_k proportional to (-alpha k^2 + beta k^4)|Phi_k|^2,
alpha>0, beta>0,
```

selects `k_*^2=alpha/(2 beta)` and
`ell_*=sqrt(2 beta/alpha)`. An effective radius potential

```text
V_eff(R) = -A/R + B/R^n + C R^m,
A,B,C>0, n>1, m>0,
```

can bound finite-energy motion from both below and above. These controls prove
only that competing orders can select finite scales. A relativistic
implementation must independently avoid higher-derivative ghosts—preferably
through a second-order auxiliary regulator unless degeneracy is proved—and
derive rather than insert its terms.

The local causal gate is sharper. For an affinely parametrized, irrotational
null congruence,

```text
d theta/d lambda = -theta^2/2 - sigma_ab sigma^ab - R_ab k^a k^b.
```

A trapped-to-expanding transition requires the restoring sector to overcome
the expansion and shear terms for a finite interval, or to derive why a
different microscopic causal equation applies. A coordinate scale-factor
minimum is not that proof. Nor is lower closure sufficient: the same solution
must provide an upper closure, asymptotic operational completion, or finite
handoff with finite quasi-local state data. An unbounded genealogy can then be
an unending **process** of finite transformations without becoming a completed
infinite state or a perpetual-motion engine.

### Nested Gradient Spectrum and scale flow

The spectral language is not merely metaphorical. QFT expands fields in modes
of a declared operator/background, spectral functions encode response, and
effective field theory plus renormalization organize descriptions by
resolution. Thermodynamics supplies state occupation, free energy, transport,
and entropy. NGS asks whether an FGC transition defines an actual mode-transfer
map

```text
g_(i+1)(omega) = T_i{g_i}(omega) + Delta g_i(omega),
```

with a finite change in accessible mode count and a dimensionless frequency or
spacing prediction. Proper frequency must be defined relative to an observer,
so a universal SI-hertz increment is not invariant.

Competing gradient, higher-gradient, nonlinear, boundary, and possibly
dissipative terms can select a finite band of structure. Dissipation is not a
universal prerequisite: conservative and unitary systems also generate complex
correlations, while friction can either stabilize or erase a pattern. The
general test is whether the equations derive the instability, saturation,
restoring response, and entropy production claimed.

The golden ratio is retained only as a candidate eigenvalue. If the derived
cross-level scales obey `L_(i+1)=L_i+L_(i-1)`, their asymptotic ratio satisfies
`q^2=q+1` and hence `q=(1+sqrt(5))/2`. Friction by itself does not produce that
recurrence. The transfer dynamics must derive it before the ratio is compared
with a spectrum or population.

NGS-0 records these three mathematical controls with explicit nonclaims. It
does not derive the recurrence coefficients, domain ensemble, cross-level mode
operator, or any physical dark component.

An additional NGS hypothesis asks whether a degree of freedom observed in a
daughter domain as a particle could be a pole, resonance, or spectral band
induced by coupling to modes of an inherited or adjacent phase. That is a
possible change of ontology, not a denial of the measured phenomena. Quark and
Higgs signatures, masses, decays, scattering amplitudes, confinement behavior,
and detector responses remain compulsory evidence. A deeper account would
have to derive a transition kernel and daughter spectral density whose poles,
widths, residues, charges, spin/statistics, unitarity, and Lorentz behavior
recover those data while also freezing a new discriminator. Without that
operator and prediction, “a remnant from a previous gradient” remains an
analogy.

The introductory billiard-ball picture is therefore not the target being
defended: QFT already describes particle content through quantized field
excitations, states, correlators, amplitudes, and localized detector events.
NGS adds the unproved possibility that a common finite-gradient substrate
derives localization and persistence. It cannot assume that every excitation
is a soliton; that would require a nonlinear finite-energy solution, an actual
stability mechanism, and a quantization map recovering observed spin and
statistics. Nor does ordinary nuclear binding-energy release by itself prove a
deeper pressure medium. “Pressure” must become a derived stress tensor or
energy functional before it carries explanatory weight.

This hypothesis is deliberately outside the present SF1 calculation. SF1 uses
an independent classical scalar `chi` as a controlled collapsing stress
carrier and `phi` as the candidate regulator. It contains no neutron-star
equation of state, quark/Higgs microphysics, particle-destruction rule, or
cross-domain spectral-transfer operator.

## 2.4 The analogies—and their limits

Several analogies in the historical corpus are genuinely generative:

- **A trampoline or thin membrane** suggests an order parameter whose stiffness and curvature vary with state.
- **Cavitation and pressure chambers** suggest phase instability, nucleation, and a boundary separating regimes.
- **Acoustic horizons** demonstrate that a finite characteristic speed and effective causal cones can emerge for perturbations in a medium [16,17].
- **Quantum tunnelling** shows that a classically forbidden connection can have nonzero transition amplitude; baby-universe tunnelling has a theoretical history [15].
- **Parametric resonance and energy pumping** suggest how periodic background structure can transfer energy into selected modes.
- **Microfluidic sorting** demonstrates that gradients and interfaces can separate populations by response coefficients.
- **Biological repair and self-correcting codes** suggest feedback, redundancy, and error thresholds.

None of these is a derivation of black-hole cosmology. A fluid has constitutive equations; a membrane has material degrees of freedom; a tunnelling calculation has an action and boundary conditions; a sorter has a known force law. To graduate from analogy, each picture must identify the corresponding invariant variables, equations, and conserved quantities in the gravitational model.

This particularly affects the retired “torsion filter.” Microfluidic selection by spin is an analogy until an action produces a spin-dependent transition amplitude and a cosmological abundance. Minimal Einstein–Cartan theory does not by itself delete scalar superpartners or import neutralinos.

## 2.5 DSF-1: constants as domain settings, stated operationally

The stronger thesis is not that reality contains no stable quantities. It is
that some quantities which appear constant inside one causal domain may be
vacuum or state values selected by dynamics. Bekenstein’s covariant varying-
`alpha` programme and his general relation between physical and gravitational
metrics show that questions of this type can be formulated without abandoning
covariance or causality [42,43]. They do not establish that any such variation
occurs in Recursive Horizons.

The first discipline is categorical. Mathematical constants such as `pi` do
not vary as material couplings. The numerical values of dimensional quantities
such as `c`, `G`, `hbar`, a temperature in kelvin, or the Planck length depend
on units. A cross-domain claim becomes physical only through an operational
dimensionless comparison, for example

```text
X_domain = { alpha,
             m_e/m_p,
             G m_p^2/(hbar c),
             k_B T/(m_e c^2),
             c_T/c_gamma,
             entropy and transition ratios }.
```

The Planck length `l_P=sqrt(hbar G/c^3)` is a derived dimensional combination,
not an observed lattice spacing. Absolute zero is a thermodynamic ground-state
limit, not zero quantum energy or the disappearance of geometry. Effective
couplings can also run with renormalization scale without varying in cosmic
time. DSF-1 keeps these objects separate.

The second discipline is dynamical. For a mode `a`, an action must derive a
characteristic equation

```text
G_a^(mu nu)(Phi, nabla Phi, ...) k_mu k_nu = 0.
```

If `G_a^(mu nu)=Omega(Phi)^2 g^(mu nu)`, the null cone is conformally unchanged;
no physical speed variation follows. A nonconformal characteristic structure
can instead define an observable such as

```text
delta_a(z,k,n) = c_a/c_gamma - 1.
```

The model must derive its sign, scale, direction and frequency dependence,
recover the common observed cone in the accessible domain, and freeze its
parameters before the target data are examined. DSF-1 is therefore an
operational specification of RH-1, not an extra proof of the transition.

## 2.6 We see things for what they truly are: open-minded without madness

The project is intended to escape paradigm lock without escaping evidence.
That requires a symmetric rule: neither an inherited noun nor a preferred new
picture is allowed to do explanatory work merely because it has been named.
“Dark matter” and “dark energy” compress linked observational behavior inside
specified models; they do not, by themselves, prove a unique particle species,
literal vacuum substance, modified geometry, or extra-domain origin. But the
phenomena are not optional. CMB structure and lensing [19], the separation of
lensing mass from most baryonic mass in the Bullet Cluster [72], and the two
independent supernova programmes that established late acceleration [73,74]
are among the constraints a replacement must jointly recover. ΛCDM plus GR is
therefore the empirical comparator wherever it works—not a protected ontology,
and not a baseline this programme may ignore.

The anti-epicycle constraint is **no unaccountable explanatory variable**, not
“no latent variable.” Physics routinely infers fields and parameters before
direct particle detection. For every proposed degree of freedom `q`, the model
must instead publish a variable ledger,

```text
q = {observable/latent/nuisance/gauge/interpretive status,
     units and invariant outputs,
     measurement or transition map,
     symmetry redundancy and prior domain,
     calibration data, held-out target, falsifier},

y = H(q,theta) + measurement error.
```

An added physical branch must change at least one predeclared operational
distribution,

```text
p(D_*|M_1) != p(D_*|M_0),
```

and the target `D_*` cannot be the same information used to invent or tune the
branch. A named boundary force, cross-domain pull, hidden gradient, or freely
chosen function that never changes a held-out prediction is not an explanation.
Nor is a model simpler merely because it replaces two nouns with one
unconstrained relation. A unified dark-sector claim must reproduce both the
matter-side CMB/BBN, clustering, lensing, growth, structure, and halo burdens
and the acceleration-side supernova, BAO, CMB-distance, growth, local-gravity,
stability, and propagation burdens with one action and parameter ledger.

Antimatter marks an important evidentiary boundary. The positron was observed
directly [75]; antihydrogen is produced, trapped, and spectroscopically tested
[76], and its free fall has been observed to be consistent with ordinary
terrestrial attraction at present precision [77]. The open cosmological
problem is the matter--antimatter abundance asymmetry, together with sharper
CPT and equivalence tests—not whether antimatter is an unresolved dark noun.

The word “relational” is used in an interpretation-neutral operational sense.

# 3. Causal speed, photons, and the meaning of space

## 3.1 `c` is not produced by light sources

# 4. What general relativity actually contributes

## 4.1 A black-hole interior is cosmological in a precise sense

For `r<r_s`, the classical Schwarzschild coordinate `r` is timelike in character and the Schwarzschild interior can be written in a Kantowski–Sachs form [9]. In that limited geometric sense, an interior is a contracting cosmological spacetime rather than an ordinary static room.

This is a substantial clue, but three inferences do not follow:

The child-universe statement is the proposed extension, not the coordinate transformation.

## 4.2 The Hubble-sphere compactness identity

For a flat FLRW model, define the critical density and Hubble radius as

```text

```

The critical mass-energy inside a sphere of radius `R_H` is

```text

```

Therefore

```text

```

This equality is exact because the same definitions and Friedmann relation appear on both sides. It is not a measured coincidence and it is not sufficient evidence that the universe is a Schwarzschild black hole. The Hubble radius is not generally an event horizon, the observable-universe radius is much larger than `c/H_0`, and FLRW lacks the asymptotically flat exterior assumed by the Schwarzschild compactness criterion. In spherical cosmology the Misner–Sharp mass and apparent-horizon language is more appropriate.

The identity remains useful: it explains why horizon-like algebra naturally appears at the cosmological expansion scale. Its correct label is **identity**, not **proof**.

## 4.3 Why a black hole does not consume every available energy density

Continued collapse does not create infinite total mass. The classical
singularity is a statement of geodesic incompleteness and divergent curvature
in an extrapolated solution, not a material object containing an infinite
amount of mass. The exterior remains characterized by finite conserved or
asymptotic charges such as mass, angular momentum, and charge. Far-away matter
can orbit, escape, join other bound systems, or remain outside causal contact.

A black hole grows when energy-momentum or gravitational radiation actually
crosses the relevant horizon. Dynamical-horizon balance laws relate horizon

The compact-mass attraction and positive-`Lambda` acceleration are equal at

Cancelling the common factors yields
