One active root owns scientific and publication work. Never use an old nested
checkout or a second package installation to make a current check pass.
Historical replay is explicit through `scripts/replay_history.py`.

Claims retain assumptions, domain, evidence and uncertainty. Spherical symmetry
motivates a candidate; it does not prove nesting. Borrow known results. New
calculations require the existing admission record and a finite budget; hold
assumptions fixed within each test. A failed route leaves others open. The paused
incoming-gate campaign and stopped RN runs are not compulsory work.
Choose calculations by their prerequisites and downstream unlocks. Known equations
often need compatible data or a physical identification before they can be used;
recomputing a known component without changing that gap is not progress.
