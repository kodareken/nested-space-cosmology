## Evidence labels

- **Postulate.** A requirement of the Nested-Space model. It orders construction. It is not a theorem and not an observation.
- **Imported result.** Established mathematics, physics, or measurement used as a launch surface. Binding it here is not novelty.
- **Repository derivation.** An identity, closure, or exact reduction obtained in this repository from stated premises.
- **Numerical diagnostic.** A computed number or scan whose compact JSON forbids promotion to a physical inheritance scale, particle mass, or cosmology.
- **Open prediction.** A quantity that would become a result only if it is derived from the frozen operator without using the later compared datum.
- **Interpretive hypothesis.** A picture useful for reasoning. It is not an action, a spectrum, a solution, or an observation.
