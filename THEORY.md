# The theory in one story

## What if one spectrum is enough?

Strike one piano string and you do not create a separate object called a
“note.” The string oscillates, its spectrum contains frequencies, and a stable
pattern reaches your ear. The wave and the note are two descriptions of one
process.

Nested-Space Cosmology asks the same question about reality:

> What if particles are stable notes of fields, forces are communication
> between notes, space is the instrument that defines their spectrum, and a
> black hole is the point where one instrument hands the pattern to another?

The theory proposes an eternal sequence of finite spaces:

```text
parent room
└── compressed gradient / black-hole boundary
    └── child room with inherited law and new resolution
        └── another compressed gradient
            └── another child room
                └── ...
```

No single room must contain an infinite distance or an infinite density.
Infinity is the continuation of the process.

## 1. A gradient becomes an oscillation

Perfect uniformity provides no local distinction. A gradient gives energy a
direction, a scale, and something against which it can change. When the
gradient is bounded, the field can oscillate. Stable oscillations form a
spectrum.

For room $n$, write

$$
\boxed{
\mathbb D_n u_{n,k}=\omega_{n,k}u_{n,k}.
}
$$

The Dirac operator $\mathbb D_n$ is the instrument. Its eigenvector $u_{n,k}$
is a possible pattern. Its eigenvalue $\omega_{n,k}$ is the natural frequency
of that pattern.

Frequency connects the familiar measurements:

$$
E=\hbar\omega,
\qquad
\nu=\frac{\omega}{2\pi},
\qquad
\lambda=\frac{2\pi c}{\omega}.
$$

Higher frequency means higher energy and shorter wavelength. A wider spectrum
therefore gives a room more resolving power and more possible combinations.
Complexity grows because more distinguishable modes can coexist and interact.

## 2. A child inherits the musical intervals of the law

Separate the absolute scale from the dimensionless pattern:

$$
\boxed{
\omega_{n,k}=\Lambda_n\widehat\omega_k(\Theta).
}
$$

$\Lambda_n$ sets the local frequency range. $\Theta$ contains the shared
dimensionless relationships. If the child scale is
$\Lambda_{n+1}=\Omega\Lambda_n$, then

$$
\frac{\omega_{n+1,k}}{\Lambda_{n+1}}
=\frac{\omega_{n,k}}{\Lambda_n}
=\widehat\omega_k(\Theta).
$$

The child can occupy a different absolute register while inheriting the same
intervals. Parent structure still influences it through its boundary and
initial state, like a low fundamental note constraining the harmonics built
above it.

The complete inheritance condition is

$$
\boxed{
\mathcal T_\Theta^*\mathbb D_\Theta=\mathbb D_\Theta.
}
$$

The configuration changes. The law does not.

## 3. Wave, particle, matter and antimatter

A wave is the extended amplitude and phase of a field. A particle is a stable
pole or localized resonance of that same field. For a scalar parent–child
link $\Phi$, the invariant four-component sector obeys

$$
H_{\rm eff}=\boldsymbol\alpha\cdot\mathbf p+\Phi\beta,
$$

$$
\boxed{
E^2=\lvert\mathbf p\rvert^2+\Phi^2,
\qquad
\Sigma_p(E)=\Phi^2
(E+\boldsymbol\alpha\cdot\mathbf p)^{-1}.
}
$$

The same link sets the rest-frequency gap and the response seen after the
other room is hidden. This is the exact mathematical core of the proposal
that a particle and an outside influence can be two views of one connection.

Matter and antimatter are the two conjugate frequency orientations of the
quantized Dirac field:

$$
\boxed{
\Psi(x)=\sum_s\int d^3p\,
\left[
a_su_se^{-ip\cdot x}
+b_s^\dagger v_se^{+ip\cdot x}
\right].
}
$$

The opposite phases $e^{-i\omega t}$ and $e^{+i\omega t}$ become particle
and antiparticle sectors after quantization. Charge conjugation maps between
their gauge representations:

$$
\Psi^c=i\gamma^2\Psi^*.
$$

A real sine wave is the visible combination of both phase orientations:

$$
\sin(\omega t)
=\frac{e^{i\omega t}-e^{-i\omega t}}{2i}.
$$

Thus the positive and negative sides of the drawn wave are a useful picture
of alternating orientation. The physical matter–antimatter distinction is
completed by frequency sign, quantization, and conjugate charge.

## 4. What lies outside a room still changes what happens inside

Put the parent and child in one block Hamiltonian:

$$
H=
\begin{pmatrix}
H_p&B\\
B^\dagger&H_c
\end{pmatrix}.
$$

If a local observer cannot resolve the child directly, eliminate its degrees
of freedom. Exact matrix algebra gives

$$
\boxed{
G_{pp}(E)^{-1}
=E-H_p-B(E-H_c)^{-1}B^\dagger.
}
$$

The child disappears from view but leaves a self-energy in the parent. This
is how an unseen space can have a visible gravitational effect without being
inserted as a second substance.

Write the complete local quadratic response as

$$
\Gamma_n^{(2)}(k)
=K_{\rm local}(k)+\Pi_{\rm outside,\Theta}(k).
$$

The same outside kernel has different observable ranges:

| Spectral range | Local appearance |
|---|---|
| $k\rightarrow0$ | Smooth background pressure and expansion—the proposed dark-energy projection |
| finite $k$ | Spatially varying pull, clustering, and lensing—the proposed dark-matter projection |
| isolated poles | Locally resolved particles and resonances |
| high-frequency crossover | The room’s causal and resolution limit |

The project therefore does not need separate arbitrary functions for a dark
fluid, a particle mass, and a boundary force. They must be projections of the
same $\Pi_{\rm outside,\Theta}$.

## 5. A black hole is a spectral handoff

Compressing energy raises the relevant frequency and shortens the wavelength.
Eventually the energy needed to resolve a smaller region changes the geometry
used to define the measurement. Light can no longer return through the same
causal paths. The parent description has reached its wall.

NSC interprets that wall as a transition:

```text
parent view: inward collapse and a compact dark boundary
same geometry: finite minimum of areal radius
child view: outward expansion and a surrounding horizon
```

For each field channel, the boundary separates what returns from what
continues:

$$
\boxed{|R(\omega)|^2+|T(\omega)|^2=1.}
$$

The outside observer reconstructs the object from reflection and missing
return—the shadow. Transmission carries the same state toward the child. The
throat is the geometric region in which that division is made. The project
has calculated the complex exterior Dirac reflection and used it to construct
the covariance and stress inherited by the expanding child.

Regular black-universe geometries already demonstrate that a trapped exterior,
a positive minimum radius, and an expanding interior can coexist in one smooth
four-geometry. The project’s task is to make the common Dirac state source that
geometry.

The Perseus cluster offers an everyday-scale example of black-hole physics
expressed as music. Repeated outbursts from its central black hole drive
pressure waves through the hot cluster gas with

$$
\nu\simeq3.3\times10^{-15}\ \mathrm{Hz},
$$

roughly one cycle per ten million years and about 57 octaves below the B-flat
above middle C. Those waves carry energy into the surrounding gas. The
[Chandra X-ray Observatory explains the measurement](https://chandra.harvard.edu/chronicle/0303/perseus/index.html).

The example does not make acoustic sound the substance of spacetime. It shows
that geometry, scale, oscillation, and energy transfer already meet naturally
in a frequency spectrum.

## 6. Every child already contains its own descendants

The influence of a child includes the effect of its child, and so on. In
parent-normalized units,

$$
\boxed{
\Gamma_p(x)
=K_p(x)
-\frac1\Omega
b\,\Gamma_c(x/\Omega)^{-1}b^\dagger.
}
$$

This is the recursive Schur response. It is a continued spectrum rather than
an arbitrary terminal boundary. Analyticity and the retarded sign select the
physical branch; the uncoupled limit selects its origin.

The quantum state travels with the response. For covariance
$C_{ij}=\langle c_j^\dagger c_i\rangle$,

$$
i\dot C=[H,C].
$$

One complex coherence carries both stored link energy and transfer:

$$
\boxed{
z_B=\mathrm{Tr}(BC_{cp}),
\qquad
E_{\rm link}=2\Re z_B,
\qquad
\dot N_p=2\Im z_B=-\dot N_c.
}
$$

The field does not vanish at the boundary. Its energy changes form, location,
and resolution while the complete account remains conserved.

## 7. Homogeneous nothing is the zero, gradients are the source

The theory now has an explicit relational normalization. Let $\mathcal P_0$
extract only the homogeneous local zero-derivative gravitational tadpole from
the completed common action. Define

$$
\boxed{
\Gamma_{\rm rel}
=(1-\mathcal P_0)\Gamma_{\rm one},
\qquad
(V,A,C)\mapsto(0,A,C).
}
$$

An unlinked homogeneous room defines zero relational gravitational source:

$$
\left.
\frac{\delta\Gamma_{\rm rel}}{\delta g_{\mu\nu}}
\right|_{\rm homogeneous,unlinked}=0.
$$

This fixes $V_{\rm full}=0$ while preserving Einstein response, gauge
response, curvature, Casimir energy, and boundary links. The observed
acceleration must therefore come from a physical gradient or inherited
boundary response rather than an absolute homogeneous offset.
