\section{Unresolved regions as local sources}
\label{sec:intro}

A local observer in one nested region does not resolve every degree of
freedom that still influences the local field. Write a finite quadratic
Hamiltonian in parent/child blocks,
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:block-H}
  H
  =
  \begin{pmatrix}
    H_{AA} & B \\
    B^{\dagger} & H_{BB}
  \end{pmatrix}.
\end{equation}
Eliminating the complementary block is ordinary linear algebra. The
retained resolvent is the inverse of the Schur complement
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:schur}
  S
  =
  K_A - B K_B^{-1} B^{\dagger},
  \qquad
  G_{AA}
  =
  S^{-1}
  =
  \bigl((\lambda I-H)^{-1}\bigr)_{AA},
\end{equation}
where $K_A=\lambda I-H_{AA}$, $K_B=\lambda I-H_{BB}$,
and $\lambda$ is a complex spectral parameter outside the relevant spectra.
The Feshbach self-energy is
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:feshbach}
  \Sigma_A(\lambda)
  =
  B(\lambda I-H_{BB})^{-1}B^{\dagger}.
\end{equation}
The complementary region disappears from the local basis and leaves an
effective operator on the retained block.

In time, the same elimination is not a memory kernel alone. The
complementary initial state survives as an inhomogeneous source
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:memory}
  i\dot\psi_A
  =
  H_{AA}\psi_A
  -i\int_0^t B\,e^{-iH_{BB}(t-s)}B^{\dagger}\psi_A(s)\,ds
  +B\,e^{-iH_{BB}t}\psi_B(0).
\end{equation}
The integral is retarded. The last term carries $C_{BB}(0)$ and the
initial cross block $C_{AB}$. Occupation data live in that remainder; they
are not selected by $\Sigma_A(\lambda)$. Nested-space cosmology interprets this
triple---reduced operator, retarded memory, retained initial
state---as the local source associated with an unresolved nested region.
Inside and outside are relational roles of the inheritance map
$\mathcal T_{\Theta}$, not assignments of gauge charge.

The present article turns that interpretation into a declared local test on
an incoming surface $\Sigma$. A metric history $g$ transports a
\emph{fixed} upstream source to an incoming kernel $C_{\Sigma}[g]$. Two
constraint residuals, generated by lapse and shift variations, are then
asked to vanish on a connected interval $I$ of positive length. The
laboratory already owns the chart, the source inventory, the retarded
evolution, and a nine-component error interface. What it does not own is a
closed certificate. The generated local verdict occupying that interface is
\textbf{\NSCVerdict}.

\section{Imported mathematics}
\label{sec:imported}

The local calculation reuses published mathematics inside the domains
stated by those authors. Application to nested-space cosmology requires the
connection that is actually shown.

Klich~\cite{Klich2003} proves a Fock-space trace identity that reduces
certain traces of quadratic fermionic operators to single-particle
determinants, and thereby recovers Levitov's full-counting-statistics
formula. The published chapter is a contribution to mesoscopic quantum
noise. The identity is used here as established algebra for a Gaussian
fermionic functional. It does not select an occupation, close
\eqref{eq:tolerance}, or supply a cosmological source.

G\'{e}rard, H\"{a}fner and Wrochna~\cite{GerardHafnerWrochna2023} construct
the Unruh state for massless Dirac fields on slowly rotating Kerr
spacetimes. In their stated Kerr--Kruskal domain, Theorem~1.1 proves that
the state is asymptotically thermal at Hawking temperature on the past
event horizon and is pure and Hadamard in the union of exterior and
interior regions. Affine-frequency horizon data and incoming-vacuum data at
past null infinity belong to that theorem. The theorem does not
automatically prove the characteristic traces, spin/current maps, or
Hadamard hypotheses on the nested-space black-universe chart used here.

Bronnikov, Dehnen and Melnikov~\cite{BronnikovDehnenMelnikov2007} describe
regular black-universe geometries: an exterior resembling Schwarzschild, a regular
minimum of areal radius, an expanding Kantowski--Sachs interior, and a
de~Sitter infinity in place of a singularity. Those metrics and causal
extensions are reusable geometric examples. Their supporting phantom source
is not the selected Dirac / closed-time-path source of the local gate.

Blanes and Moan~\cite{BlanesMoan2006} construct commutator-free Magnus
integrators. Equation~(43) of that paper is the fourth-order two-exponential
scheme
\begin{equation}
\label{eq:cf4}
  x_h
  =
  \exp(\alpha_1 h A_1+\alpha_2 h A_2)
  \exp(\alpha_2 h A_1+\alpha_1 h A_2)\,x_0
\end{equation}
used as a numerical method on the extended linear system when a time
integrator of that family is compared. Formal order is not an error
certificate for~\eqref{eq:tolerance}. Production evaluations use the owned
DOP853 path; CF4 is a comparison method with its own implementation
controls.
