\begin{abstract}
We give an explicit finite-operator construction exhibiting four nested
qualities: coupled regional composition, an inherited law under a normalized
scale transformation, preserved dimensionless spectral relationships, and
an effective local response to unresolved regions. Every finite depth is
covered by the same algebraic argument. The transformation acts on an indexed
family of equal-depth windows, not by an unscaled dilation of a single fixed
matrix. Admissible quantum states may differ between regions; common laws
do not require identical occupations or behavior. Ordered Schur reduction
retains the influence of a region's own surroundings, while time-domain
elimination retains memory and initial-state information. These results reuse
standard spectral and block-operator mathematics and connect the existing
Nested-Space Cosmology formulation to a concrete model. They establish
mathematical nested qualities within the stated finite class, without changing
local quantum evolution. A separate gravitational incoming-gate calculation
remains OPEN and is reported only as an application checkpoint.
\end{abstract}

\section{Question and relation to the foundation}
This article is a focused companion to the preserved Nested-Space Cosmology
working manuscript, version 0.26.0~\cite{EkNSC2026}. Its organizing relation is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:inheritance}
\mathcal T_\Theta^*\mathbb D_\Theta=\mathbb D_\Theta.
\end{equation}
The intended question is whether coupled regions can inherit a common law,
while having different local states and retaining the influence of their
surroundings in a reduced description. Here we specify a finite construction
and a transformation for which these properties can be proved.

A region is a declared subspace of the finite system. Calling one region an
inside and its complement an outside describes their relation in the chosen
partition. It does not assign gauge charge or assert a particular horizon
geometry. A common operator law is compatible with different initial states,
occupations and correlations. A property of one region is therefore not
assumed to be the property of every region.

The contribution is the connected construction and its precise interpretation.
Schur elimination and unitary spectral covariance are established mathematics,
not discoveries claimed here. We do not need to reconstruct published
black-universe geometries to establish these finite properties. Such
geometries provide separately scoped examples, with their own supporting
sources~\cite{BronnikovDehnenMelnikov2007}. Nor does the present theorem
require a proof of eternal dynamics or closure of the numerical application
in Appendix~\ref{app:gate}.

\section{An inherited family of coupled regions}
Fix a finite regional dimension $d$, a Hermitian prototype $H$, a nonzero
$d\times d$ coupling $B$, and a finite positive scale ratio $\Omega$.
Use orthonormal regional frames and common energy units with $\hbar=1$.
For an integer starting label $m$ and a finite depth $N\geq1$, let
$\mathcal H_{m,N}$ be the direct sum of $N$ copies of $\mathbb C^d$.
The domain is the whole finite space. Define the Hermitian matrix $J_{m,N}$ by
\begin{align}\label{eq:window}
 (J_{m,N})_{jj}&=\Omega^{m+j}H, &&0\leq j<N,\\
 (J_{m,N})_{j,j+1}&=\Omega^{m+j}B,
 & (J_{m,N})_{j+1,j}&=\Omega^{m+j}B^\dagger, &&0\leq j<N-1,
\end{align}
with all other blocks zero. The nonzero link makes the construction coupled.
The shared data are $\Theta=(H,B,\Omega)$; $N$ specifies a boundary problem.
The exterior remainder after the first region is removed is exactly the
window $J_{m+1,N-1}$ when $N>1$.

\begin{proposition}[Inheritance under normalized relabeling]\label{prop:inherit}
Let $U_m:\mathcal H_{m,N}\to\mathcal H_{m+1,N}$ be the unitary map that
relabels corresponding ordered regional frames. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:shift}
 J_{m+1,N}=\Omega U_mJ_{m,N}U_m^\dagger,
 \qquad \Omega^{-1}U_m^\dagger J_{m+1,N}U_m=J_{m,N}.
\end{equation}
In particular,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:spectrum}
 \spec\!\left(\Omega^{-m}J_{m,N}\right)=\spec(J_{0,N}).
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Every nonzero block in~\eqref{eq:window} gains one factor $\Omega$ when
$m$ increases by one. The unitary $U_m$ changes its label, not its matrix
entries in corresponding frames. This proves~\eqref{eq:shift} block by
block. Iteration and invariance of spectrum under unitary conjugation give
\eqref{eq:spectrum} for every integer $m$ and finite $N$.
\end{proof}

Equation~\eqref{eq:shift} gives a concrete meaning to the pullback in
\eqref{eq:inheritance}: relabel the window and remove its declared scale.
The invariant object is the indexed law/family. We do not assert that a
nonzero finite matrix is unitarily equivalent to an unscaled dilation of
itself. For fixed $N$, the frequency eigenvalues can be written
$\omega_{m,k}=\Lambda_m\widehat\omega_k(\Theta,N)$ with
$\Lambda_m=\Omega^m$ in the chosen units. Isolated regional blocks obey the
same statement without the $N$-dependence.

This does not make the dressed spectrum independent of surrounding regions.
Changing $N$ changes the boundary problem and can shift that spectrum.
Likewise, the theorem does not determine a physical value of $\Omega$ or
identify $H,B$ with a particular curved-spacetime operator. Those are explicit
inputs or separate identifications, not quantities inferred from notation.

\section{Nested reduction retains the exterior influence}
For a complex spectral parameter $z$ with $\operatorname{Im}z>0$, all required
resolvents exist: the finite Hamiltonian and its principal subblocks are
Hermitian. For a retained block $A$ and complement $E$, write
\begin{equation}
 J=\begin{pmatrix}J_{AA}&V\\V^\dagger&J_{EE}\end{pmatrix}.
\end{equation}
Its retained resolvent is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:schur}
 [(zI-J)^{-1}]_{AA}
 =\left[zI-J_{AA}-\Sigma_A(z)\right]^{-1},\quad
 \Sigma_A(z)=V(zI-J_{EE})^{-1}V^\dagger.
\end{equation}
The unresolved region is absent from the retained basis but remains in its
response. This is exact elimination, not removal of a physical influence.

\begin{proposition}[Composition at every finite depth]\label{prop:schur}
Starting at the final region, define
\begin{align}
 S_{N-1}(z)&=zI-\Omega^{m+N-1}H,\\
 S_j(z)&=zI-\Omega^{m+j}H
 -\Omega^{2(m+j)}B S_{j+1}(z)^{-1}B^\dagger.
\end{align}
Then $S_0(z)^{-1}$ is exactly the first regional block of
$(zI-J_{m,N})^{-1}$. Grouping the exterior regions before eliminating them
gives the same result.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
The two-block inverse identity~\eqref{eq:schur} establishes the terminal
elimination. Apply it successively to the resulting complement; induction
on $N$ gives the claim. Alternatively eliminate the entire complement in
one step. Both procedures equal the same block of the joined inverse,
which proves agreement independently of the grouping.
\end{proof}
For three blocks of $K=zI-J$, this statement is particularly visible:
\begin{equation}
 K=\begin{pmatrix}A&-V&0\\-V^\dagger&C&-W\\0&-W^\dagger&F\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad S=A-V(C-WF^{-1}W^\dagger)^{-1}V^\dagger.
\end{equation}
The outer contribution enters the middle response, which enters the retained
response. Noncommuting products keep their displayed order.

Normalize $S_j$ by $\Omega^{m+j}$ and set $x=z/\Omega^{m+j}$. The recurrence is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:recursion}
 \Gamma_j(x)=xI-H-\Omega^{-1}B\Gamma_{j+1}(x/\Omega)^{-1}B^\dagger.
\end{equation}
This is the repository's existing normalized chain. The factor $1/\Omega$
follows from the scale of the eliminated inverse; it is not an adjustable
coupling. The proof covers arbitrary finite depth. No assumption about an
infinite physical lifetime enters it.

\section{The same law permits different regional states}
Let $C$ be any Hermitian fermionic one-particle covariance satisfying
$0\leq C\leq I$. Different regional blocks can have different occupations
and cross-region correlations. Under the same static $J$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:state}
 C(t)=e^{-iJt}C(0)e^{iJt},\qquad i\dot C=[J,C].
\end{equation}
Unitary conjugation preserves the interval $0\leq C\leq I$, but does not
require equality of regional blocks. For example $C_{\rm stat}=I/2$ is
stationary, whereas a nonuniform diagonal $C_{\rm var}$ with
$[J,C_{\rm var}]\ne0$ evolves under the identical operator. Within that
second state the regional occupations already differ. This is a statement
about admissible states, not a simulation of stable stars or collapse.

Solving the exterior Schr\"odinger equation and substituting it into the
retained equation yields
\begin{align}\label{eq:memory}
 i\dot\psi_A={}&J_{AA}\psi_A
 -i\int_0^t V e^{-iJ_{EE}(t-s)}V^\dagger\psi_A(s)\,ds\nonumber\\
 &+V e^{-iJ_{EE}t}\psi_E(0).
\end{align}
The initial exterior term must remain. Its covariance contains
$V e^{-iJ_{EE}t}C_{EE}(0)e^{iJ_{EE}s}V^\dagger$; cross correlations also
retain $C_{AE}(0)$. Thus the retarded operator and the state-dependent
response are different objects. Equal operator laws need not produce
equal measured histories.

Total energy is conserved for static $J$:
\begin{equation}
 \frac{d}{dt}\Tr(CJ)=-i\Tr([J,C]J)=0.
\end{equation}
Regional energy can nevertheless be exchanged. With the existing allocation
$h_A=(PJ+JP)/2$, the current is $i[J,h_A]$. This reuses finite quantum
energy accounting and does not introduce a second independently chosen
source. The Gaussian formulation can use established fermionic determinant
identities~\cite{Klich2003}; a metric-source interpretation still requires
the applicable common-action variation.

\section{Verification, interpretation and scope}
The analytical arguments establish the four qualities for the declared
finite construction. A new small control record binds this proof to exact
rational complex matrices: three two-dimensional regions, $\Omega=3/2$
and $z=1+2i$. Twelve exact predicates check the normalized shift, joined
versus nested inverse, recurrence and distinct admissible states. Removing
the normalization, reversing the noncommuting coupling or omitting the
state contribution is detected. These are bounded integration checks;
they do not replace the arbitrary-depth argument with a numerical depth scan.

The source and record are included under \texttt{paper/nested-quality-evidence}
in the repository. The pinned source is lab commit
\texttt{4b3c44d0c47a49a17f543ff19e0298770ac041e8}.
The replay command is
\begin{quote}\small\ttfamily
python paper/nested-quality-evidence/scripts/\\
check\_nsc\_nested\_qualities.py --check
\end{quote}
It requires SymPy; the ordinary publication hash verifier does not.
Earlier correspondence and recursion records are retained as authenticated
references, with no claim that their whole gravitational dependency chain
has become a new closed certificate.

This construction makes nested qualities mathematically explicit using
standard local finite dynamics. It offers a precise way to describe an
unresolved surrounding system through its local response. Identifying that
response with cosmological dark matter or dark energy, or deriving numerical
values of $c$ and $\Lambda$, is not established by the Schur identity alone.
Those identifications are not required for the result proved here.

The term \emph{self-similar law} refers to the specified normalized
transformation. It is not a measurement of a spatial fractal dimension of
the Universe. An infinite geometric construction would need its own domains
and limiting state; an eternal engine is neither assumed nor proved.
Different regional states remain a feature of the construction throughout.
