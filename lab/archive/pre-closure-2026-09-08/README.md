# Recursive Horizons

## A thermodynamic research programme for black-hole-to-cosmology transitions

**Popular subtitle:** *Can Einstein and Hawking Finally Rest Easy Together?
Here Is What Must Be Proven*

Recursive Horizons asks whether a collapsing black-hole interior can pass
through a finite, nonsingular transition and become an expanding causal domain
while preserving the successful local physics of general relativity, quantum
field theory, thermodynamics, and ΛCDM.

The answer is not known. This repository is a conjectural, falsifiable research
programme—not proof that our universe is inside a black hole, not a completed
theory of quantum gravity, and not an observational detection.

The canonical organizing hypothesis is
**[Finite Gradient Closure](docs/finite-gradient-closure.md)**. It treats a
singular endpoint or completed physical infinity as a boundary the retained
description has failed to complete. An unbounded succession of finite domains
is logically different: every realized state and handoff must remain finite,
constraint compatible, flux balanced, causally defined, and subject to the
appropriate entropy law.

Its spectral specialization,
**[Nested Gradient Spectrum](docs/nested-gradient-spectrum.md)**, asks whether
a controlled transition changes finite mode density, bandwidth, complexity,
or coarse-grained stress. It does not identify dark matter, dark energy,
particle ancestry, a universal hertz increment, the golden ratio, or inevitable
observers.

The project’s posture is **open-minded without madness**. ΛCDM plus GR is the
empirical comparator where it works, not protected ontology and not evidence
that a replacement may ignore CMB, lensing, clusters, structure, expansion,
local gravity, or multimessenger constraints. New fields, gradients,
boundaries, or relational variables must have an operational map, a changed
predeclared prediction, and a falsifier.

## The idea in one page

The programme asks for a derived causal chain:

```text
independent matter collapse
  -> finite invariant activation threshold
  -> healthy faster-growing restoring sector
  -> finite trapped-region response
  -> positive complete affine-null Raychaudhuri margin
  -> finite expanding continuation
  -> upper closure or finite handoff.
```

The decisive local observable is the complete twist-free null Raychaudhuri
right-hand side

```text
Q = -theta^2/2 - sigma_ab sigma^ab - R_ab k^a k^b.
```

A positive mechanism result requires a finite interval inside a genuinely
trapped region with `Q` positive by more than its complete error budget,
agreement between direct expansion differentiation and independent curvature
assembly, three resolutions, two methods, finite fields and invariants, and no
earlier branch, kinetic, hyperbolicity, constraint, scale, boundary,
conservation, affine, or null stop.

Spherical symmetry makes the screen shear zero. That is a specialization, not
evidence that a nonspherical transition survives shear.

Even a robust local positive result would not yet prove a global child
spacetime. A later FGC-3 result must close the exterior, junction or handoff,
quasi-local mass and flux, generalized entropy, global causal continuation,
rotation, shear, and any inner-horizon stability burden.

## Reproduce the active checks

Create an environment with the dependencies in `pyproject.toml` and the pinned
paper/evolution requirement files, then run:

```bash
make verify
make verify-fgc-sf1-foundation
make verify-fgc-tdg10-qa2-pref1
make data
make paper
```

Long campaigns and live binders are explicit operations and are absent from
ordinary verification. Do not invoke a `run-*`, `resume-*`, bootstrap,
recovery, status, or `--live` path without reading its exact owner and
authority.

`make verify-fgc-sf1` is reserved for the eventual result-bearing outer proof.
It currently fails with an incomplete-status message so a green foundation
cannot be mistaken for DEF1/ROB1 completion.
