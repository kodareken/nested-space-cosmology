# FGC-1-TDG9-AR1-PREF1: independent arithmetic-discriminator binder

PREF1 independently binds the completed, ignored TDG9 AR1 two-leaf raw
namespace. It imports neither the AR1 runner nor the TDG8 persisted-retry
wrapper. From the authenticated generation-`9`, `10`, and `11` checkpoints it
reconstructs the generic TDG6 full/two-half/four-quarter proposal sets, proves
their durable rejection records are byte-identical, extracts all eighteen
owned binary64 Hermite channels, and repeats both exact evaluators under the
frozen resource ceilings.

The independent result reproduces all `108` evaluator calls. Exact sufficient
contraction failures occur in ten retry/channel pairs: `u:alpha` and `u:R` at
retry `3`; `u:alpha`, `u:lambda`, and `u:R` at retry `4`; and `u:alpha`, `u:v`,
`u:lambda`, `u:R`, and `q:R` at retry `5`. Therefore the legacy binary64
enclosure was **not the sole owner** of nonadmission on these three frozen
finite-dimensional samples.

This is a bounded numerical diagnosis. It is not a GR-0 calibration result,
does not prove continuum or asymptotic order loss, and says nothing about
FGC-QR, SGB-L, gradients, a transition, or physics. No successor remedy is
selected. A later prospective design must decide whether these exact sampled
failures justify reformulating the temporal diagnostic, changing the numerical
method, or terminating this formulation branch.

The live binder authenticates the AR1 raw leaves and the complete PREF2 store
before and after replay. Ordinary verification consumes only the tracked
compact result and stays store-blind.
