# FGC-1-TDG10-QA1-FRZ1 — exact complete-C all-channel qualification freeze

## Decision

`FGC-1-TDG10-QA1-FRZ1` prospectively freezes one bounded numerical
qualification of the immutable retry-3 GR-0 proposal. It does **not**
authorize a campaign-state advance.

The qualification changes exactly two numerical owners relative to the
historical rejected proposal:

1. the explicit temporal tableau on the inherited RK4-2049 high-order SBP4
   spatial state is SSPRK3; and
2. every D01/D12 magnitude is enclosed from the exact radius-free complete-C
Hermite interpolant with two independent rational localizers, rather than
with TDG6's globally aggregated binary64 coefficient envelope.

The physical equations, GR-0 data, spatial operator, source, projector,
centre treatment, constraints, health checks, accepted time, retry-3 width,
grid, and temporal threshold remain unchanged.

The unchanged discriminator is

\[
8U_{12}^{2} \le L_{01}^{2},
\]

including equality. It is the sufficient observed-order condition
`p >= 3/2`. All 18 complete-state channels remain an all-of gate.

## Correct retry boundary

Retry 3 starts from the generation-9 checkpoint
`eb6fddc480c94aa7ed15c80399fc2b26637693efef02399f267075fc6c258e56`,

## Why qualification and state adoption are separate

AC1 sampled the unchanged production classifier on all 18 channels and left
`u:R` unresolved. TI2/LOC2 and UR1 then showed that the original RK4 proposal
really fails and that the old global envelope cannot resolve SSPRK3-on-SBP4
`u:R`; neither artifact qualified exact complete-C as a production owner for
all 18 channels.

The first all-18 exact measurement therefore cannot also become the first
accepted post-restart state. TDG10-QA1 may reconstruct the seven shadows,
bind their exact evidence, and serialize the complete fine endpoint as a
diagnostic object. It may not install that object into a campaign, advance a
cursor, change a ledger, or call it accepted.

If and only if an independent QA1 binder confirms an all-18 pass, a later
`FGC-1-TDG10-RA1-FRZ1` may bind the already-known endpoint and evidence hashes
and authorize one atomic adoption. That separation prevents a first-look
measurement from irreversibly writing the state it is supposed to qualify.

## Exact interval contract

For every channel and each of the 2,044 owned radial rows, one outer, two
medium, and four fine binary64 Hermite segments are reconstructed exactly
with `float.as_integer_ratio()`. The parent cubic is restricted exactly to
the child half before subtracting the child cubic. Only the complete
difference cubic enters admission:

\[
C=C_{\mathrm{restricted\ parent}}-C_{\mathrm{child}}.
\]

The primary derivative-monotone localizer and the independent
endpoint-deflated discriminant localizer must agree on polynomial and
candidate counts, stationary-root counts, survivor identities, overlapping
survivor enclosures, and overlapping global intervals. Their intersection is
the certified rational interval. Route disagreement, malformed input, or a
resource ceiling is fail-closed and cannot be rounded into a pass.

All three 1/2/4 paths must share the same bitwise initial state **and** the
same freshly evaluated initial RHS. Every child boundary must likewise share
both its state and RHS with the preceding segment. Inputs are finite,
C-contiguous little-endian binary64 arrays; narrower floating types are not
silently promoted. At the production 2,044-row shape, the D01/D12 ceilings
and depth are fixed in the adapter itself. Both exact localizers must also
report that no absolute tolerance or boundary clipping was used.

The exact interval is an enclosure of the continuous cubic interpolant of
finite binary64 shadow records. It is not the exact PDE history, a global PDE
error bound, a trajectory-order theorem, or a physical observable.

The candidate ceilings are per channel: 16,352 for D01 and 32,704 for D12.
The primary refinement depth is 160. No cross-channel sharing, absolute
tolerance, signal normalization, debit cancellation, or post-outcome
threshold change is permitted.

## Authorized diagnostic transaction

The one authorized run:

1. authenticates the sealed store and generation-9 retry-3 cursor;
2. restores the finite `RK4-2049` predecessor without mutating the store;
3. constructs seven SSPRK3 shadows on the inherited SBP4 operator;
4. reconstructs and classifies all 18 exact complete-C row streams;
5. if and only if all 18 channels pass, serializes compact exact evidence and
   a content-addressed diagnostic copy of the complete fine endpoint; and
6. proves the historical store is unchanged.

When present, the fine diagnostic object must include the finite `u,p,q` arrays, grid,
tracer positions and histories, step/transaction identity, executed
four-quarter plan, monitor and causal states, and the prospective exact debit
vector. It remains an unaccepted QA object in every terminal class.

No TDG6 envelope decision may authorize the diagnostic result, and
`commit_tdg6_gr0_compositor` is not a permitted state-write route.

## What a pass would establish

A pass establishes only that the frozen SSPRK3-on-inherited-SBP4 retry-3
proposal satisfies the unchanged all-18 complete-C temporal discriminator
under the newly qualified exact interval owner. It identifies one finite fine
endpoint that a separately frozen adoption may consider.

It does not establish:

- an accepted state, common event, or eligible GR-0 calibration;
- production SSPRK3 comparator status or two-method agreement;
- SGB-L or FGC-QR execution;
- regulator activation, trapping, or defocusing;
- singularity resolution, a child domain, or a global transition;
- retained-EFT validity or any physical result.
