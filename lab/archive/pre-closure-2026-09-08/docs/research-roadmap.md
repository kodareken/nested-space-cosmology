## Fixed scientific gates

The finish line is a conjunction. No attractive component substitutes for
another.

### Resolved regulator activation

For each held-out FGC-QR case,

```text
A_phi = max_measure |phi| / max |phi_seed|.
```

With frozen activation-error enclosure `E_A`, require:

```text

A_phi,FGCQR - E_A,FGCQR
  > max(A_phi,GR0 + E_A,GR0,
        A_phi,SGBL + E_A,SGBL).
```

### Genuinely trapped geometry

With one fixed future-null orientation, both physical-metric expansions must
separately satisfy

```text

theta_- < -4 E_theta-
```

at the required samples. GR-0 calibration requires eight consecutive common
events. DEF1 independently requires trappedness throughout its measured affine
interval.

### Complete defocusing

The decisive quantity is

```text
Q(lambda) = -theta^2/2 - sigma_ab sigma^ab - R_ab k^a k^b.
```

Require

```text
min_I Q > 4 E_Q > 0
Delta lambda / L0 >= 1/64
```

for at least eight consecutive affine samples, together with:

- direct expansion differentiation versus independent Raychaudhuri assembly
  within `1e-8`;
- affine-normalization residual below `1e-10`;
- observed order `p >= 3/2`;
- the same positive sign on three nested resolutions and both methods;
- finite fields and invariants throughout; and
- no earlier branch, kinetic, hyperbolicity, constraint, scale, aliasing,
  conservation, affine, null, or boundary stop.

Spherical shear is recorded as zero by symmetry. Nonspherical robustness is a
later independent gate.

### Mass and flux


and error enclosure must come from the branch equations before holdout access.
