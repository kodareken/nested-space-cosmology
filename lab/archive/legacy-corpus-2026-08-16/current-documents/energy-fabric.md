## 0. The Axioms, Formally

1. **Fabric.** The only content of reality is energy. Matter, fields, and spacetime itself are configurations of one fabric — the Einstein–Cartan spin fluid. Spacetime is not a container; it is the thermodynamics of horizons. The ECKS action is the fabric's constitutive equation.
2. **Instrument.** The only instrument that reads the fabric is thermodynamics. Every closure in this corpus is an entropy or temperature identity: \(\Lambda = 3/r_s^2\) (saturated entropy transfer), \(S_{\rm parent} = S_{\rm dS}\) (bit-for-bit conservation), \(T_{\rm GH}/T_H = 2/\sqrt{\Omega_\Lambda}\) (the temperature ladder), the warm band.
3. **Dynamism.** Nothing is static. Every level is flow: the drain, the bounce, the string of rooms. A static state would be a zero-entropy state — see axiom 4.
4. **The forbidden nothing.** \(S = 0\) does not exist. Every causally closed boundary saturates \(S = A/4\ell_P^2\); the point-singularity (infinite energy in zero volume) and the void (zero energy) are the same forbidden object — *nothing* — and nature refuses both. The refusal is visible: the singularity is elongated into the spaghettified string and turned by the spin-torsion bounce (P4, verified). **The modulation of energy is the signature of the forbidden nothing.**
