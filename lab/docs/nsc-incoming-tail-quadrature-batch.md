## Physical and numerical ownership

inventory, matching factor and actual endpoint. It does not relabel a channel
as22 to bypass a guard. Zero-angular groups13 and23 use one sign and the full
group factor; their thermal sphere component is exactly zero. Other thermal
components remain positive and retain lossless binary interval endpoints.

Each directed integral is compared with the unchanged archived group order13
float. The existing radial physical-tail bounds are imported independently,
and the elementary thermal tail is replayed outward. The record also encloses
the difference between the archived aggregate float and the exact sum of its
group floats. Thus final aggregation covers that additional rounding step.

The source values remain unchanged. The certificate concerns the original
order16/ad4 infinite-tail recipe and the declared affine-horizon vacuum.
It does not certify finite-offset low-energy fields, low/subgap integration,
the angular/compact complement, a physical constraint root or metric evolution.
