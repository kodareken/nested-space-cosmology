## What is imported from nonlocal quantization

[Briscese et al. (2024), sections 3.2 and 4.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.14056v2)
distinguish a nonlocal diagrammatic inverse from the ordinary time-ordered
correlator of physical states. The latter distinction is used here; neither
an entire factor nor an unchanged pole licenses replacing the state
correlator by the regulated inverse.

[Briscese–Modesto (2019), introductory assumptions](https://arxiv.org/html/1803.08827v3)

UV-growing kinetic form factors. Our reconstructed kinetic multiplier
exp[-Ein(z)/2] decreases on the positive Euclidean axis. That theorem is
therefore not imported for this profile. The guarantees above follow
directly from the canonical factor and a real branch phase.

## Derive the full-frequency vacuum vertex

Use the existing unit-lapse radius family H(J)=H0+J(t)V, with a common
Hermitian involution beta anticommuting with H0 and V. In the canonical
Euclidean frame,

\[
Q=(\partial_\tau+H)^\dagger(\partial_\tau+H)
=-\partial_\tau^2+H_0^2+J\{H_0,V\}+J^2V^2-\dot J V.
\]

Both the J² contact and the time-derivative vertex are retained. In the
H0 eigenbasis write e_i for the energies, v_ij for V, z=nu² and

\[
\beta_{ij}(\alpha,z)
=(1-\alpha)e_i^2+\alpha e_j^2+\alpha(1-\alpha)z.
\]

Expanding the proper-time trace to quadratic order and integrating its
internal frequency gives the conversion kernel

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal B_2(z)={}&\frac{1}{2\sqrt\pi}
\sum_i(V^2)_{ii}\int_0^{\Lambda^{-2}}ds\,s^{-1/2}e^{-se_i^2}\\
&-\frac{1}{4\sqrt\pi}\sum_{ij}[(e_i+e_j)^2+z]|v_{ij}|^2
\int_0^1d\alpha\int_0^{\Lambda^{-2}}ds\,s^{1/2}
e^{-s\beta_{ij}(\alpha,z)}.
\end{aligned}
\]

At finite spatial dimension these bounded proper-time integrals define
an entire function of z. Consequently the real-frequency vertex is
unambiguous in this construction:

\[
\mathcal B_{2,L}(\omega)=\mathcal B_2(-\omega^2)\in\mathbb R.
\]

The Lorentzian branch-action kernel is minus this value because the
static convention is S_L=-integral dt E. This is a bare geometric
vertex, not a retarded matter correlator. Its reality does not prove
metric causality. Its full frequency dependence is retained rather than
replaced by a finite derivative series.

The static conversion and its linear geometric force are also explicit:

\[
\mathcal B_0=\tfrac12\sum_i
[\Lambda e^{-e_i^2/\Lambda^2}/\sqrt\pi
 +|e_i|\operatorname{erf}(|e_i|/\Lambda)],
\quad
\mathcal B_1=\tfrac12\sum_i\operatorname{sgn}(e_i)
\operatorname{erf}(|e_i|/\Lambda)v_{ii}.

At fixed finite spatial rank the reference canonical sea energy is
