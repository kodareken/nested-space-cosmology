## Reconstruct the Gaussian kinetic function

Use the established identity in [NIST DLMF 6.2.3–6.2.4](https://dlmf.nist.gov/6.2):

\[
\operatorname{Ein}(z)=\int_0^z\frac{1-e^{-t}}t\,dt
=E_1(z)+\log z+\gamma_E.
\]

Ein is entire and Ein(0)=0. For the self-adjoint Euclidean representative
D of the preceding [domain construction](nsc-compact-anomaly-bridge.md),
define

\[
F_\Lambda(D)=\exp[-\tfrac12\operatorname{Ein}(D^2/\Lambda^2)],
\qquad\boxed{K_\Lambda(D)=D F_\Lambda(D).}
\]

For finite rank N, nonzero eigenvalues and the independent normalization
scale M, the scalar identity applied spectrally gives

\[
\boxed{-\log|\det(K_\Lambda(D)/M)|
=\tfrac12\operatorname{Tr}E_1(D^2/\Lambda^2)
 +N[\log(M/\Lambda)+\gamma_E/2].}
\]

The right side is exactly the existing normalized `RegulatedOperator`
action. Its last term is retained. It cannot be silently discarded when
varying the cutoff or normalization.

At fixed N, M and Lambda,

\[
\frac{d\log K_\Lambda(d)}{dd}=\frac{e^{-d^2/\Lambda^2}}d,
\qquad
\delta\Gamma=-\operatorname{Tr}
\left[e^{-D^2/\Lambda^2}D^{-1}\delta D\right].
\]

The trace identity holds for noncommuting perturbations by spectral
functional calculus; it is the previously implemented regulated source
variation. At fixed D and M the kinetic determinant instead obeys

\[
\partial_\Lambda[-\log|\det(K_\Lambda/M)|]
=\frac{\operatorname{Tr}e^{-D^2/\Lambda^2}-N}{\Lambda}.
\]

Adding N/Lambda restores the derivative of the raw heat action. No change
of units is identified with a physical scale deformation here.

\[

Although W and F_s need not commute,

\[
A^\dagger D_0 A=K_\Lambda(D_s),\qquad
\boxed{-\log J_{\rm Grassmann}
=\operatorname{Tr}\sigma
 +\tfrac12\operatorname{Tr}\operatorname{Ein}(D_s^2/\Lambda^2).}

## A normalized continuum modulus

Let M_Lambda=Lambda exp(-gamma_E/2). Functional calculus gives

\[
\left[K_\Lambda(D)/M_\Lambda\right]^2
=\exp[-E_1(D^2/\Lambda^2)].
\]

If E1(D²/Lambda²) is trace class, the right side differs from I by a
trace-class operator. Its Fredholm determinant therefore defines

\[
\boxed{\Gamma_{\rm heat}
=-\tfrac12\log\det_F\exp[-E_1(D^2/\Lambda^2)]
=\tfrac12\operatorname{Tr}E_1(D^2/\Lambda^2).}
\]

A gapped elliptic operator on a compact development geometry satisfies
the relevant ultraviolet trace condition. Zero modes, noncompact volumes
and relative determinants require their stated separate prescriptions.
This identity normalizes the positive modulus without an unrestricted
trace of the identity. It does not determine the determinant phase or
replace a physical real-time density operator.

keeps its signs. Commuting invariant projectors and an anticommuting
grading are inherited by K(D). For a unitary identification U between

\[
