# Reuse existing formulations; compute the missing connections

The full six-part objective remains. Execution starts from published
formulations and existing project evidence. Established results are reusable
inputs with stated assumptions, not a checklist of derivations to repeat.

## Before allocating scientific compute

1. Identify the closest primary-source equation or existing implementation.
2. State what can be reused, including its action, state, units and domain.
3. Name the exact NSC quantity or connection that remains unevaluated.
4. Run the smallest calculation that can resolve it. Check a convention or
   implementation only when that check can change the next decision.

If the proposed work only adds confidence in an unchanged known result,
stop and return to the missing physical connection. More tests, records,
precision or derivations do not themselves advance physical closure. Reuse
completed verification; repeat an expensive check only after a relevant
change, failure or newly identified concern. Use one integrated check at a
coherent implementation boundary.

Delegated work follows the same rule. Each brief identifies the prior result,
the new gap, owned files and bounded verification. Parallel workers must not
repeat the same literature survey, baseline derivation or full test suite.

## Compact geometry to mass: reuse the dimensional reduction

The [compact mass map](nsc-compact-mass-map.md) applies conformal Dirac
covariance and the established KK reduction to the actual free carrier.
The full spacetime warp cancels in its classical canonical field, yielding
m_n=n pi/(2 L_star) and one chiral zero mode for the declared interval domain.
The remaining interface is its interacting, state-defined realization and
physical compact size. Do not repeat intrinsic spatial spectrum scans or
the generic KK derivation to answer those questions.

For the next interaction map, use
[Castillo-Felisola et al.](https://arxiv.org/html/1405.0397v1), equations13
and18–20. Their higher-dimensional torsion contact and Clifford decomposition
are reusable; their chosen zero-mode profiles, truncation and effective
coupling do not determine the NSC massive tower's overlap coefficients.

That [overlap calculation](nsc-compact-interaction.md) is now evaluated.
Reuse its full fermionic vertex and the nonzero 3111 coupling. An imported
single-field axial-source equation needs a controlled reduction of this
tower; repeating the known KK or Einstein–Cartan derivation does not supply it.

The [five-dimensional UV match](nsc-torsion-uv-map.md) also reuses
Pfäffle–Stephan Proposition5.4, which already holds in arbitrary dimension.
Its matching-window weights and quadratic derivative term are checked. The
finite relative coefficient c_T-9c_R remains a separate matching condition;
do not repeat the leading heat calculation to determine it.

## The next useful compatibility comparison

The [action/source comparison](nsc-torsion-source-compatibility.md) now records
the coefficient conversion and the remaining domain/state conditions. Reuse
that comparison rather than restarting the same literature derivation.

It also identifies the existing Lucat–Prokopec microscopic in-in source and
backreaction equations. Those replace a generic 2PI derivation task; the
remaining work is the NSC parameter/state/domain match and its validity.

Torsion in spectral geometry is also prior art.
[Iochum, Levy and Vassilevich](https://arxiv.org/abs/1008.3630) give bulk and
boundary spectral-action terms with skew torsion and chiral bag conditions.
[Pfäffle and Stephan](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1101.1424), Theorem5.7, derive
torsionful spectral equations; their torsion equation has a Proca form.
Thus that action cannot automatically be identified with algebraic
Einstein–Cartan torsion.

Compare those existing actions and domains with the current torsionless
NSC operator before building another collapse calculation. Import any
applicable terms and coefficients, preserving their assumptions. An
extension must have an explicit version and the same parameter ownership;
it must not add a second independently weighted gravitational action.

The spin-fluid collapse paper uses a phenomenological particle-production
coefficient. Determining the corresponding source from our identified
quantum state would be new integration work. Repeating the paper's collapse
derivation would not supply that missing source.

## Apply the rule to all six targets

| Target | Existing mathematical base | Actual NSC work |
|---|---|---|
| Constants and thermal limits | Dirac cones, spectral gravitational terms, Planck-unit definitions and thermal states | Values and dimensionless relations from the common parameter set |
| Black-hole/child continuation | Regular geometries and source-specific collapse/bounce formulations | Source/action/domain compatibility and consequences of actual changes |
| Dark sector | Covariant stress, bulk-boundary gravity and cosmological perturbation equations | The operator-derived source and independent observable predictions |
| Particle/wave/measurement | QFT states, detector models, interference and correlation methods | The identified excitation/detector interaction and any additional probability claim |
| Mass and antimatter | Dirac mass/chirality algebra and charge conjugation | The physical interaction, selected sector, charges and spectrum |
| Recursion and infinity | Operator recursion, state construction and convergence methods | The common-law fixed point, physical measure and observable/time limits |

The same policy covers nuclear and stellar benchmarks: reuse the known
models; derive their coefficients from the common action before claiming
NSC predictions. The [published prior-art ledger](https://github.com/kodareken/nested-space-cosmology/blob/v0.3.0/docs/prior-art-and-open-claim.md)
contains the wider source index.

Mathematical reuse does not require re-proving the literature. A claimed
identification with NSC still requires checking the particular assumptions
and source relations on which that identification depends.
