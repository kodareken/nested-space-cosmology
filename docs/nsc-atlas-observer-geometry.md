The atlas must distinguish a coordinate description from a measured change.
This entry connects Douglas's questions about stretching, orbital descent
and fading light to explicit geodesic and metric calculations. The

fixed controls and exact identities, not a new fit to the sky.

## A stretched geometry can approach isotropic expansion

Lambda_star=.220983024213, H=sqrt(Lambda_star/3)=.271405615400 in model units.
The same vacuum equations admit the anisotropic metric

$$
ds^2=-dt^2+\sum_i a_i(t)^2dx_i^2,
\qquad
a_i=A_i[\sinh u]^{1/3}[\tanh(u/2)]^{p_i-1/3},\quad u=3Ht,
$$

where sum p_i=sum p_i^2=1. This is the Kasner--de Sitter family. Related
anisotropic constructions are used in
[Minamitsuji](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1205.5600); here all independent Ricci
components are checked directly, rather than borrowing an observational
interpretation. The existing golden Kasner triple
(-.3090169944,.5,.8090169944) is used as a declared shear-state comparison.
The action has not selected those initial exponents or our current epoch.

The directional expansion and shear satisfy

$$
\frac{H_i}{H}=\coth u+3(p_i-1/3)\operatorname{csch}u,\qquad
\sigma^2=3H^2\operatorname{csch}^2u,\qquad
\frac{\sigma^2}{3\bar H^2}=\operatorname{sech}^2u.
$$

The directional rates approach one common expansion rate. That does not
force a material boundary to become a round balloon: its shape and the
constants A_i retain separate information. The generic early Kasner

## Orbiting and spiralling are different geodesic questions

A central mass curves spacetime. An orbit can nevertheless keep constant
areal radius without an external balancing mass. In the isolated
Schwarzschild test-particle limit, G=c=1,

$$
\dot r^2=E^2-V(r),\qquad
V(r)=\left(1-\frac{2M}{r}\right)
\left(1+\frac{\ell^2}{r^2}\right).
$$

The two circular conditions V=E^2 and V'=0 give

$$
\ell^2=\frac{Mr^2}{r-3M},\qquad
E^2=\frac{(r-2M)^2}{r(r-3M)}.
$$

At r=10M, ell^2/M^2=100/7 and E^2=32/35. V'' M^2=.00114285714>0,
while the spacetime curvature invariant is K M^4=.000048>0. Thus a stable
circular geodesic exists in a curved one-mass geometry with no Andromeda or
other balancing source. This is a calculation of an ideal conservative
orbit, not a claim that real Galactic orbits remain unchanged forever.
Inward drift requires its own trajectory and energy/angular-momentum budget.

Near a Schwarzschild horizon, radial tidal separation obeys approximately
Delta a=2GM L/r^3 for a small freely falling object. It is finite and
continuous at r=2GM/c^2. Size, strength and trajectory determine when an
object is disrupted; the horizon is not an additional material pressure step.

## Frequency, distance and light speed

Distance is defined by the metric; scale factor a is a ratio of geometric
scales, not a wavelength. A massless geometric-optics probe on isotropic
FLRW gives f proportional to 1/a and physical wavelength proportional to a.
Their product is the locally measured propagation speed. If a doubles,
frequency halves and wavelength doubles while the local speed is unchanged.
With conserved photon number in a comoving volume and no absorption or
emission, radiation energy density falls by 1/16. Fading alone is therefore
not a measurement of a changed propagation speed.

The coordinate radial speed is dr/dt=c/a. A varying coordinate speed is
already allowed by the same metric. A changing locally measured speed or
dispersion relation would instead require a different probe operator or a
specified medium. The shortest supported wavelength, longest mode and
thermal state remain separately derived quantities; absolute zero is not
identified with a universe-sized wavelength by the definition of a.

Every observed frequency comparison uses the invariant

$$
1+z=\frac{(-k_\mu u^\mu)_{\rm emit}}
{(-k_\mu u^\mu)_{\rm receive}}.
$$

At the Galactic centre, S2's measured spectrum combines source motion,
gravitational redshift and transverse Doppler shift. The last two together
were about 200 km/s divided by c at the 2018 close passage, as reported by
the [GRAVITY collaboration](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.09409). That measurement
does not by itself establish Solar-System inspiral or the proposed global
drain. A nested interpretation must reproduce the same received-frequency
and orbital data using a common source and observer geometry.
