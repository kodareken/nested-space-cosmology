positive-energy row of group14, angular sign +1, and its explicit
negative-energy/opposite-angular partner. It reuses all original panels,
source coherences, weights and multiplicities. The operator is not rerun.

Each original three-column source fiber is continued once from the reference
incoming slice to the fixed upstream slice by the existing homogeneous Dirac
owner. That immutable input is saved and then applied to the already evolved
operator. This continuation does not depend on the trial history. The signed
partner uses the original negative-frequency source covariance and the
owned S3-conjugation map; no positive result is doubled.

geometry once. It retains both cross terms and the quadratic matter change,
as well as the numerical difference between the jointly evolved and cached
homogeneous reference. The saved record reports the two signed family changes
and their partial N,beta constraints on `I=S(1)+[.12,.18]`, with the full
retarded derivative of the control's one history amplitude.

The finite quadrature extends to its original endpoint,160, and includes the
existing low/subgap rows. Their numerical/source accuracy is still unknown.
The other nonzero-angular families, the changed-history infinite tail, field
and interpolation error, and the between-node remainder remain explicit OPEN
requirements. This control is neither a root search nor a local gate result.
The three exact ell=0 changes are zero and their baseline allocations remain.

Replay checks the saved upstream inputs, original source identity, operator
and history bindings, both constraint arrays and the recorded measurements.
It performs homogeneous reference contractions but no field PDE solve or
source-preparation reconstruction. No physical parameter, source convention,
stress term or metric timestep is introduced.
