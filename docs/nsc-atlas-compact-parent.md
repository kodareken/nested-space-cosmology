# A compact shared parent and what its observers can distinguish

The [normal-clock parent](nsc-atlas-parent-evolution.md) established an
evolving two-level geometry but retained an isolated asymptotically Euclidean
end. Its large-domain average therefore diluted to zero. This construction
replaces that end by a second physical interior, using the same membrane law.
It produces a complete compact slice, an explicit common future, computed
regional volume fractions and a nonzero change under additional nesting.
It also proves that this global average cannot be equated with the local
Lambda inferred by the retained cap observer.

## Construction and premises

Retain the existing centre-to-outer-throat part of the nested body.
It contains the inner cap, inner membrane, .01 ell Kottler host and
left outer membrane. Its outer vacuum taper ends at R=ell and reaches
the Schwarzschild throat R=.8 ell, with connector mass M=.4 ell.

On the right attach one of two specified interiors:

- **Regular completion:** the original one-level de Sitter cap, wall at
  R=1.5 ell, k=2/ell, and Schwarzschild mass .4 ell.
- **Nested completion:** a reflected copy of the existing nested body,
  including its inner .01 ell Kottler region and inner wall at .8 ell.

The first compact state has three walls; the second has four. Both have
two regular centres. The interiors differ in internal structure. A
spherical connected vacuum region has one conserved Schwarzschild mass,
so this is not a construction of two independently adjustable connector
masses. In the compact geometry M is a regional mass invariant, not an
ADM mass at infinity.

H*ell=1 in every nonzero-vacuum region and H=0 in the outer connector;
k*ell=2 and phi=0 remain unchanged. The regional vacuum energies,
wall radii, masses and original slicing profiles are supplied premises.
There is no fitted volume fraction or target cosmological constant.
Use L_ref=2/k=ell and the original normal-clock interval
0<=t/ell<=.01.

This is a new global completion in the same piecewise theory. It does
not claim that an evolving isolated body changes its topology into this
completion. The [calculation admission](prior-art-and-open-claim.md#compact-parent-and-its-observer-restriction-20-october-2026)
compares it with lattice/Swiss-cheese and infinite-hierarchy routes;
the compact construction supplies an explicit connector with no new
coupling or matter source at the join.

## Reflection gives a compatible compact geometry

Let x increase from a body's own centre toward its parent throat. The
global radial label lambda runs from the left centre to the right centre.
On the right, lambda reverses x while the future normal N is preserved:

$$
E_{\rm global}=-E_{\rm local},\quad V_{\rm global}=V_{\rm local},
\quad A_{\rm global}=A_{\rm local},\quad
B_{\rm global}=B_{\rm local}.
\tag{K1}
$$

E is the signed spatial radius derivative. Its label derivative E_x is
even under this reflection. Thus the intrinsic curvature, expansion,
shear and both scalar source projections are unchanged.

At the right membrane the global minus side is the connector and the
global plus side is the cap/child. Reverse both the local normal and the
side order. Then

$$
\beta_-^{g}=-\beta_+^{loc},\quad
\beta_+^{g}=-\beta_-^{loc},\quad
b_g=-b_{loc},\quad \gamma_g=\gamma_{loc}.
\tag{K2}
$$

Substitution gives the same physical junctions,
[E]_g=-kR gamma and [V]_g=kRb_g, and the angular and temporal Israel
jumps are both -k. Surface energy stays positive.
For the regular right interior, at R=1.5 ell,
beta_minus_local=437/360 and beta_plus_local=-643/360.
The global spatial slopes are 2.027786089806 on the connector side and
-1 on the cap side. The future wall areal derivative stays
1.650310950872. These signs retain the future, rather than gluing one
body to a time-reversed copy.

Both vacuum tapers reach the same static Schwarzschild collar at R=ell.
In its signed bridge chart R=2M+y^2, the initial R and geometric data
are even in y, E is odd, and V=B=0. The two halves therefore agree on
open extensions through the throat. There is no new curvature or source
atom at y=0. Their normal evolution agrees there by uniqueness of the
same regular radial ODEs. This is an explicit local isometry, rather
than an appeal to an unevaluated many-body connector theorem.

The radial interval has positive S2 fibres in its interior and a
regular collapse of those fibres at both endpoints: R=0, |E|=1 at
each centre. Its underlying manifold is S3. The metric is smooth away
from the declared material walls and has the existing junction
regularity at them. There are zero asymptotic ends and zero arbitrary
outer boundaries. The integrated Hamiltonian is the sum of the
already satisfied bulk and wall constraints; it adds no new equation
that selects the cap H or membrane scale.

This uses the same thin-wall dynamics and spherical branch conventions as
[Aguirre and Johnson, Section II](https://arxiv.org/html/gr-qc/0508093v2)
and the [owned source construction](nsc-atlas-connection-slice.md).
Closed shell-supported universes have other published constructions,
for example [Lemos and Luz](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.105.044058);
their static matter law is not imported here. No priority claim is made
for compact shell topology or the Schwarzschild reflection.

cap-to-host and connector-to-host crossing strips.

Reflection preserves the positive A bound. The minimum host radius obeys
the previous first-exit bound through .01 ell; the connector throat obeys
R>=.799875 ell on that interval. The right regular wall's incoming rapidity
stays positive by the same exterior-collar estimate as the original outer
wall. Thus the halves share a finite evolving slice. The two centre clocks
are proper clocks with a common initial leaf; no physical signal
synchronization between the centres is implied.

Let M_L and M_R now denote the seven **moment vectors**, rather than
the connector mass. Each contains its own physical wall atoms once:

$$

## A global change with exactly the same local clock signal

The two compact states have identical left data, connector mass and
left wall motion. They differ only in the right interior. At t=.01 ell,

$$
\Delta\Lambda_{g,\rm tr}\ell^2=-.181930519744,\qquad
\Delta\Lambda_{\rm total,tr}\ell^2=.040940072190.
\tag{K5}
$$

The left inner-cap flash still has
t_rec/ell=1.646160028498 and 1+z=3.895009111788 in both states.
The equality follows from the retained local geometry and causal
separation, not from limited numerical sensitivity.

The shared connector fixes that causal statement explicitly. Put
T=0 on its original static bridge and
rstar=R+2M log(R/(2M)-1).
The two taper integrals give, in ell units,
T_left=-.804805873579 and T_right=.804892000619 for the regular
completion. On its exterior branches define

$$
U=\epsilon e^{-(T-r_*)/(4M)},\qquad
W=-\epsilon e^{(T+r_*)/(4M)},\qquad
-UW=(R/(2M)-1)e^{R/(2M)} ,
\tag{K6}
$$

where epsilon=+1 on the left and -1 on the right. The symbol W here
is a null coordinate, not a wall or a moment.
Future causal curves have dU>=0,dW>=0. The left outer wall has
U>0,W<0, and the right one U<0,W>0. A future path cannot move from
one exterior to the other. Radial null paths are already the fastest;
angular motion does not supply an alternative.

The initial inward null fronts also cannot have a common reception
event in this connector. They would require

$$
UW\geq U_{L,0}W_{R,0}
=15.6040388088>1
\tag{K7}
$$

for the regular completion, and 15.6031988754 for the nested one.
The physical Schwarzschild domain R>0 has UW<1.
This argument is specific to those wall emission events and the
constructed connector. It does not assert that arbitrary observers'
future light cones never overlap.

Consequently these two solutions provide a counterexample to identifying
the whole compact scalar average with the locally measured cap Lambda.
The right nesting changes the global mean while the cap's local
curvature and retained flash observations remain identical. Compactness
removes dilution; an observer-dependent physical reduction still needs
its causal/state mechanism.

## A precise continuation question at the quiet throat

The finite regular construction does not remove the current quiet
Kottler endpoint. At the small host throat the normal has E=V=0
initially. Its future radius obeys

$$
R_t^2=\frac{2M_h}{R}+H^2R^2-1.
$$

For its inward branch, put R=rb sin^2 chi and a=H^2 rb^2.
The proper lifetime to R=0 is the regular integral

$$
\tau_b=2r_b\int_0^{\pi/2}
\frac{\sin^2\chi\,d\chi}
{\sqrt{1-a(1+\sin^2\chi+\sin^4\chi)}} .
\tag{K8}
$$

It gives tau_b/ell=.0314434597796445, with exact bounds

$$
\frac{\pi r_b}{2\sqrt{1-a}}
\leq\tau_b\leq
\frac{\pi r_b}{2\sqrt{1-3a}},
\tag{K9}
$$

numerically [.031434800637,.031447397238] ell. At H=0 the
formula recovers pi M. Because the Kottler curvature contains
48 M_h^2/R^6, this exact quiet solution cannot continue the whole
normal foliation smoothly through that event. The calculation does
not establish that no other obstruction happens earlier, or that a
physical universe changes topology or ends. It specifies a failure of
this classical continuation and a concrete replacement problem.

In the same Einstein framework, use the spherical mass
m=R(1-|grad R|^2)/2, normal energy rho and radial pressure p_r.
The radial Einstein equations give

$$
\nabla_N\nabla_N R=-\frac{m}{R^2}-\frac{\kappa R}{2}p_r,\qquad
\nabla_N\nabla_N R+\nabla_s\nabla_s R
 =-\frac{\kappa R}{2}(\rho+p_r).
\tag{K10}
$$

For a geodesic normal the first Hessian is R_tt. At a temporal turn
with E=R_t=0, m=R/2. A regular bounce at a strict spatial minimum
therefore needs

$$
\kappa p_r<-1/R^2,\qquad \rho+p_r<0.
\tag{K11}
$$

At the current rb, the first threshold is
kappa p_r ell^2<-2497.9987983973. The supplied vacuum has
kappa p_r ell^2=-3. Replacing its initial turn by a bounce at the
same radius would require an added radial pressure below
-2494.9987983973 in these curvature units, with compatible density,
constraints and conservation. This necessary condition is not a
constructed source or a demonstration of a bounce. A bounce at a
different radius must satisfy the condition there; changing the
gravitational action changes the equations and defines a new candidate.

Thus the next physical proof can be stated in equations: construct a
source-derived regular continuation on the actual throat, or select a
different non-AE parent whose observer domain evolves cosmologically.
The supplied quiet cap H, nonspherical stability, formation, quantum
state and observed expansion have not been derived by compact gluing.
The playful atom/remnant interpretation is not an input to this work.
