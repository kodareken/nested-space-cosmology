# Reuse the full metric measure, not a convergence weight

The finite anomaly integrand and the remaining quantum measure are different
owners. No new scale integration is run until its measure and physical
variables are defined. In particular, a one-dimensional volume factor that
makes a toy integral finite does not determine the full common functional.

[Mottola's geometric construction](https://arxiv.org/html/hep-th/9502109v1),
sectionIII, builds a configuration-space measure from an inner product on
metric deformations and then divides out diffeomorphism orbits. The quotient
includes the Jacobian/Faddeev–Popov determinant, zero-mode treatment and the
remaining physical metric integrations. SectionIV.3 explains why the
supermetric's trace parameter is not fixed by covariance alone and must
agree with the dynamics. We should reuse this construction when the NSC
kinetic functional and domain are specified.

For the displayed supermetric, the trace eigenvalue is 1+CD/2; its zero is
C=-2/D. This follows directly from that displayed expression. Do not copy a
dimensionally inverted threshold from surrounding prose without checking
the definition.

[Dasgupta–Loll](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0103186v2), sections2–4, gives a
conditional conformal-factor cancellation in a Lorentzian proper-time-gauge
construction. It uses an Einstein–Hilbert kinetic term, a common regulator
for the partition function, an assumed renormalization treatment of volume
factors and a specified DeWitt-measure range. Its proper-time gauge and
signature continuation are not the NSC heat-kernel proper-time regulator.
The source does not evaluate all remaining physical metric integrations.

A bounded Grok review identified these conditions. Its suggestion that a
trapped region itself establishes geodesic focusing or rules out a global
proper-time gauge is not accepted: the actual congruence and domain must be
checked. A PG shift alone is not an obstruction to changing gauge.

The current five-dimensional carrier also has a compact endpoint problem
and a scale-field interpretation to resolve. Its metric conformal mode,
common Dirac compensator and relative-sheet resolution must not be silently
identified. A quotient construction needs the common action's kinetic
operator and compatible gauge/boundary conditions; a physical collective
field instead needs its actual measure. The existing conditional Weyl lift
does not choose between them.

Keep the existing normalization and Weyl-cocycle development evidence
available. Do not repeat it to select an undetermined invariant coefficient,
and do not import a measure or gauge parameter solely because it produces a
desired stationary scale. The [charged self-sourcing route](nsc-charged-self-sourcing-route.md)
is a separate reusable semiclassical source test, with its own explicit
matching requirements.
