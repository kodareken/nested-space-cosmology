# Inheritance, regional state and energy

The parent–child postulate is $\mathcal T_\Theta^*\mathbb D_\Theta=\mathbb D_\Theta$.
Its [scale realization](schur-memory-or-inheritance.md) carries one inverse
factor and one additional $1/\Omega$ per resolvent level. At level $n$ the
argument is rescaled by $\Omega^{-n}$; this operator skeleton does not identify
the factorial Matryoshka polygon count with the exponential Schröder chain
count.

On the fixed free Dirac child geometry, the relative-state bound keeps
`PARALLEL_RATIO_MINIMUM = sqrt(3*pi/2-1)`. With $s=a_\parallel/r$ and
$s_{\min}=\sqrt{3\pi/2-1}$,

$$
|\Delta\rho|\leq\frac{M+K/r}{4\pi s_{\min}r^3},\qquad
M=\sum_j|m_j|\|\Delta C_j\|_1,\qquad
K=\sum_j\left(\frac{|k_j|}{s_{\min}}+|\kappa_j|\right)\|\Delta C_j\|_1.
$$

The measure includes each physical degeneracy once. This is a homogeneous,
angularly averaged density bound on the same geometry and domain. It does
not select an absolute vacuum or bound arbitrary inhomogeneous states.

For a static Dirac lattice, regional energy assigns half of every crossing
link to each side: $H_A=\{P,H\}/2$, $H_B=H-H_A$, and the current into $A$ is
$J_A=i[H,H_A]=i[H^2,P]/2$. Expectations use $\operatorname{Tr}(CH_A)$ with
$C_{ij}=\langle c_j^\dagger c_i\rangle$. Bare regional energies plus the full
link energy give the same total; their currents differ by stored interaction
energy. Retarded elimination also needs the exterior initial state and the
initial cross covariance.

The imported MMP dictionary maps common Lorentzian coefficients
$\int\sqrt{-g}(AR-CF^2-V)$ to

$$
G_N=\frac1{16\pi A},\qquad g^2=\frac1{4C},\qquad
r_e^2=\frac{q^2C}{4A},\qquad
\ell_{\rm MMP}=\frac{16r_e^3}{|q|G_N},\qquad
\lambda_4=\frac{V}{2A}.
$$

The coefficient and domain controls do not solve the MMP geometry. Their
first unresolved common-action input is the full vacuum coefficient
$V_{\rm full}$, followed by the complete state and magnetic return path.
Original controls are at [Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23090015).
