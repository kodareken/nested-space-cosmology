## What is reused

The geometry and momentum rates are
`nsc_spherical_galerkin_coupling.compose_fine_hamiltonian`, reached through
the nested canonical frame. The canonical map remains \(a=W^{\mathsf T}g\)
and \(\pi=\Delta x_g W^{\mathsf T}p\). \(W\) is the frozen replay basis on a
stored handoff. It is not rebuilt, and `initial_state` is not called.

The observer \(V\) is the original child pair, columns \((2,3)\) of the fixed
T0 frame. It is not the evolved source and it is not a geometry-mode index.
The source \(\Phi\) is the full fermion band. There is one metric. Child and
parent clocks are samples of \(rQ\) on that metric, at \(x=1,2,3\).

The column law is \(-iH\Phi\). The multiplicity \(M=4\kappa\) stays inside
the existing nodal forces and the field energy, once. No new interaction is
added.

## Exact split

Let \(P_A=VV^{\dagger}\) and \(P_E=I-P_A\), with \(V\) fixed, so there is no
Berry term. At the opening of a segment

\[
\Phi=Va+e_{\mathrm{drive}}+e_{\mathrm{memory}},
\]

\[
\begin{aligned}
i\dot a&=Aa+B(e_{\mathrm{drive}}+e_{\mathrm{memory}}),\\
i\dot e_{\mathrm{drive}}&=P_EHP_Ee_{\mathrm{drive}},\\
i\dot e_{\mathrm{memory}}&=P_EHP_Ee_{\mathrm{memory}}+B^{\dagger}a.
\end{aligned}
\]

\(A=V^{\dagger}HV\), \(Be=V^{\dagger}He\), and \(B^{\dagger}a=P_EHV a\).
The drive starts at \(P_E\Phi(t_0)\). The memory starts at \(0\). The same
occupation weights multiply every column. The initial cross is the thin
block \(a\,\mathrm{diag}(c)\,e_{\mathrm{drive}}^{\dagger}\). It is part of
the reconstructed \(\Phi\), not a separate covariance matrix.

\(H\) is applied by the existing matrix-free FFT carrier. The exterior
propagator is not formed and not stored. A dense one-body covariance is not
formed. The admissible spectrum used for the CAR check is the spectrum of
the \(6\times 6\) weighted column Gram, which carries the nonzero spectrum
of \(\Phi\,\mathrm{diag}(c)\,\Phi^{\dagger}\).

## Volterra reading

Let \(U_E(t,s)\) be the exterior evolution generated by \(P_E H(t) P_E\).
It is an operator in the equation, not a stored history. Then

\[
\begin{aligned}
e_{\mathrm{drive}}(t)&=U_E(t,t_0)P_E\Phi(t_0),\\
e_{\mathrm{memory}}(t)&=-i\int_{t_0}^{t}U_E(t,s)P_E H(s)V a(s)\,ds.
\end{aligned}
\]

Substitution produces the retarded equation for \(a\). The kernel
\(V^{\dagger}H(t)U_E(t,s)P_E H(s)V\) is the self-energy of the two retained
amplitudes. It moves \(a\), and through \(a\) it moves the reconstructed
source. It is not a stress and it is not added to \(F_L\), \(F_Q\), or
\(F_\beta\).

The streamed RK4 step is that differential system. Because the three
equations add to \(i\dot\Phi=H\Phi\), one streamed step and one independent
full coupled RK4 step advance the same source and therefore the same force
and the same geometry.

## Same-action force

Before \(g\) and the canonical momenta are updated, the columns are
rebuilt and passed to the existing source. \(F_L\), \(F_Q\), and \(F_\beta\)
are the nodal derivatives of \(M\operatorname{Tr}(CH)\) on that full
source. The conformal rate then uses \(F_L\) and \(F_Q\) exactly as it
already does, and it keeps \(F_\beta\) as the shift-source partial. The
chart derivative of the field energy, at fixed \(\Phi\), is the contraction
of \(F_L+F_Q\). The off-chart shift derivative is the contraction of
\(F_\beta\). Both are checked by centred differences.

The quadratic force of \(Va+e_{\mathrm{drive}}+e_{\mathrm{memory}}\)
contains the cross of those three pieces. The geometry uses that full
force. The \(2\times 2\) block \(a\,\mathrm{diag}(c)\,a^{\dagger}\) is the
child modal kernel. It is not the spatial stress and it is not substituted
for the force.

## Elimination identity

On a solution of \(i\dot\Phi=H\Phi\), the representative column Lagrangian

\[
\ell=\frac{i}{2}\bigl(\Phi^{\dagger}\dot\Phi-\dot\Phi^{\dagger}\Phi\bigr)
-\Phi^{\dagger}H\Phi
\]

vanishes. Its integral, weighted by the shared occupations, vanishes with
it. A geometry variation through a family of solutions with the initial
source held fixed therefore has bulk Euler-Lagrange contraction equal to a
pure endpoint form

\[
\Bigl[\sum_k c_k\frac{i}{2}
\bigl(\Phi_k^{\dagger}\delta\Phi_k-\mathrm{c.c.}\bigr)\Bigr]_{t_0}^{t_1}.
\]

The initial piece is zero because \(\delta\Phi(t_0)=0\). The remaining bulk
integrand is \(-\Phi^{\dagger}\delta H\,\Phi\), which is the existing force
contraction. The endpoint term depends on the initial state through the
evolved variation. It is not a local source and it is not inserted into
\(F_L\), \(F_Q\), or \(F_\beta\).

The numerical laboratory freezes geometry, evolves the split field, and
checks integration by parts for an exterior test function that vanishes at
the opening and is aligned with the final exterior. The integral uses
Simpson's rule on that uniform laboratory grid. The freeze is an identity
check. It is not the coupled law, and it does not define a second induced
coefficient. The spectral induced term already present in the geometric
action is left where it is.

## Omission and the rank-12 gap

Setting the memory derivative or the drive derivative to zero after the
split is a counterfactual projection. The projected vector is not \(H\Phi\).
The projection does not define a unitary law on the full band, does not
regenerate a history by itself, and is not a gate on the full-versus-split
comparison.

A physical cross-dephasing preparation would need an admissible covariance
and a source that remains consistent with the constraints. The live force
path is a rank-6 column block. A rank-12 control is not that block. The
implementation names this as `physical_cross_dephasing_preparation` and
does not truncate the source, hardcode a rank-6 substitute, or zero the
force.

## What the short check measures

The stored inputs are the matched \(dt=0.0005\) baselines at \(T=0.3\) for
\(n_f=128\) and \(n_f=256\), together with the frozen \(W\). The optional
check advances both the independent full RK4 and the streamed split by
\(0.01\) in coordinate time, twenty stored steps. It compares the field,
the weighted-Gram spectrum, the three forces, the canonical geometry and
momenta, and the proper-time integrals of the evolved \(rQ\). The CAR
admissibility and the existing field-energy partition are recorded on that
same state.

On this creation pass the twenty-step comparison agrees at roundoff. The
largest absolute gaps were about \(2\times 10^{-16}\) in the field,
\(2\times 10^{-15}\) in the canonical geometry and momenta,
\(1\times 10^{-14}\) in the three forces, and \(0\) in the proper-time
integrals, at both \(n_f=128\) and \(n_f=256\). The initial retained-exterior
cross has Frobenius norm about \(4.7\times 10^{-2}\), and its force
contribution is about \(2\times 10^{-2}\) at \(n_f=128\) and
\(1\times 10^{-2}\) at \(n_f=256\). Over the window the field moves by about
\(6\times 10^{-2}\) and \(4\times 10^{-2}\) at the two resolutions, the
proper clock advances by about \(1.21\times 10^{-2}\), and the largest
absolute change among the canonical geometry and momentum coefficients is
about \(11\) and \(16\). Those coefficient changes are not a radius reading.
The two integrators agree on them. The weighted-Gram spectrum stays inside
the declared occupations, from about \(0.25\) to about \(0.75\). Chart,
lapse, and shift partials of the field energy match the existing forces
with gaps from about \(10^{-10}\) to about \(10^{-8}\). The endpoint term
is about \(1.73\) and is not added to the force. Drive and memory omissions
leave Hamiltonian residuals of about \(4\) to \(6\) and about \(0.11\) to
\(0.16\).

Agreement on this window is a finite RK4 comparison. It is not a continuum
certificate and it is not a production result.

The rank-12 dephasing gap remains. It is not a prerequisite for the coupled
response. The full-versus-reduced comparison runs without it.

## Segment command

The executable segment starts at the saved \(T=0.3\) state and frozen \(W\).
Its full span is duration \(0.7\), so it ends at \(T=1\). Step caps are
\(0.0005\), and optionally \(0.001\) or \(0.00025\). \(T=3\) is outside the
segment.

```sh
python3 scripts/lab.py scripts/derive_nsc_discovery_coupled_memory.py --write --duration 0.7 --output results/development/nsc-discovery-coupled-memory-v1
python3 scripts/lab.py scripts/derive_nsc_discovery_coupled_memory.py --run --output results/development/nsc-discovery-coupled-memory-v1
python3 scripts/lab.py scripts/derive_nsc_discovery_coupled_memory.py --check --output results/development/nsc-discovery-coupled-memory-v1
python3 scripts/lab.py scripts/derive_nsc_discovery_coupled_memory.py --short
```

`--write` stores the opening state, producer and source hashes, the CPU
forecast, and the 64 MiB checkpoint limit. It does not evolve the segment.
`--run` is the evolution. Root is the executor for the full span. A forecast
above the CPU budget stops before that evolution and leaves the opening
chunk. `--check` reloads the committed bytes, replays the common-action
forces, clocks, and endpoint partials from those bytes, and takes no step.
`--short` remains the \(0.01\) comparison. A new campaign may be created under
`lab/results/development/nsc-discovery-coupled-memory-*`; existing campaign
outputs and unrelated repository paths are refused.

At a completed endpoint the record keeps the evolved split, including
memory. The complementary force is the common-action force of the exterior
vector. The cross term is the shared-weight remainder. Their sum is the
effective outside force. Both the independent full state and the streamed
split are stored, and the replay compares those stored arrays.

A preparation at \(dt=0.0005\) measured one coupled step at
\(0.0443\,\mathrm{s}\) for \(n_f=128\) and \(0.0491\,\mathrm{s}\) for
\(n_f=256\). With the factor \(1.5\) the \(1400\)-step forecast is
\(92.9\,\mathrm{s}\) and \(103.1\,\mathrm{s}\), together \(196\,\mathrm{s}\),
inside the \(21600\,\mathrm{s}\) budget. That preparation did not evolve
the segment. A separate \(0.01\) campaign replayed its stored forces and
clocks with no further step.
