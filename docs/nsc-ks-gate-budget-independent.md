# History-independent budget

The declared allocations sum to `2e-11` and leave `1e-11` for the residual.
V5, the boundary buffer and the CF4 time indicator are cited by hash.
They are not a changed-history enclosure. The value-only one-knob movement
is larger than every allocation, so each component is ESCALATE. An
escalation is not a bound and not NON-EXISTENCE.

python3 scripts/derive_nsc_ks_gate_budget_independent.py --record
