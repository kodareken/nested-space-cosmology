# What this instrument cannot answer

The chain document says what was measured. This one says where the
instrument stops, because every result read off it is bounded by that and
the boundary is not the same everywhere.

Each limit below is a measurement, not a caveat. Tests read the records and
check them.

## One degree of freedom

There is no transverse mode, because there is no slot for one. The response
tangent raises unless the state is `(2, points, 1)`; the connection is
evaluated at a hardcoded `theta = 0.5 * math.pi`; and every recorded array
is one-dimensional in radius, `coupled_phi` being `(2, 869, 1)`.

The angles enter as `r^2 dOmega^2`. That factor is the circle at areal
radius `r`. Smaller `r` is the inside of that circle, and that inside is the
third dimension. The circle plus the inside is the ball. The sentence
"carries a sphere and supplies a radius" is withdrawn: it called the interior
a missing dimension.

**Bounded:** every stored array is radial. A claim that the front of the
circle differs from the back cannot be made from this chart. A claim about
the ball can, because the ball is this reduction. The gradient balance is
that claim, and it matches the sphere.

## The parent/child cut carries nothing

The construction nests a parent and a child with the cut at `r_plus +
gap/2`. Tested for a feature at the cut across the lapse, shift, redshift and
both speeds, at three mass ratios: **none**. Curvature excess runs `0.29` to
`0.83`, so every field is less curved at the cut than elsewhere on the chart.

The construction asserts a nesting and supplies no physics for it. The one
interface it does supply is at `r_plus`, which is **below** the child rather
than at its boundary.

**Bounded:** nothing here tests whether a parent/child boundary is physical.
The answer available here is that this chart is silent.

## The sourced march lives on the trapped region

The excision boundary condition requires a pure outflow. The outgoing speed
changes sign at `r_minus = 0.754343`: above it the excision absorbs, below
it it emits. The condition therefore holds strictly between the two
horizons, and **every measurement in this line was taken there**.

**Bounded:** the third region is not merely unvisited. It has no admissible
boundary condition, so a march there would be a new construction rather than
an extension of this one.

## There is no expansion, and the outward region is algebraic

The chart has no cosmological horizon. Measured over five decades, the
outgoing speed's approach to its asymptote settles on a local exponent of
`-0.5000`, the power law the closed form predicts. Nothing exponential
appears outward.

**Bounded:** nothing here speaks to dark energy, dark matter, antimatter, or
whether an expanding universe is exponentially sensitive. Those need
structure this chart does not have, and saying otherwise would be the easy
error.

## What is left, and who owns it

Two questions remain, and neither is a measurement this instrument can make.

**Is the child or contains a universe.** The child is an areal interval on
one chart with a known characteristic structure. That is a description, not
an occupancy.

**Is the nesting meant to be physical or is it bookkeeping.** The cut scan
says this chart does not know the line is there. Whether that is a defect
or the intent is a question about the construction, not about the code.

## The pattern

Three of the four limits above are the same shape: the construction carries
a piece of geometry — a nesting, a sphere, a region — and the dynamics
supply nothing behind it. That is not a criticism of the results. It is a
map of the instrument, and every number in this programme has to be read
inside it.

What the instrument *is* good for is real and substantial: the dynamics of
matter crossing a one-way membrane, how it compresses, how it relaxes, and
what it retains. That is a complete and well-controlled subject, and it is
not what 2005 answers.

## Scope

An assembly of limits already measured and recorded elsewhere. **It does not
measure the actual universe.** Its purpose is to bound the other results, not
to add one.
