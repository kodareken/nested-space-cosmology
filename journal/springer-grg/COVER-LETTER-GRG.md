### Scientific Motivation and Fit with *General Relativity and Gravitation*

Three active lines of research in mathematical relativity—many of whose foundational papers appeared in *General Relativity and Gravitation*—have independently addressed the breakdown of classical single-domain FLRW and Schwarzschild models:
1. **Isotropic singularities and conformal boundary continuation** (Goode & Wainwright 1985; Anguige & Tod 1999; Tod 2003; Tod, *Gen. Relativ. Gravit.* **47**, 17, 2015),
2. **Regular black-bounce and black-universe spacetimes** (Bronnikov, Dehnen & Melnikov, *Gen. Relativ. Gravit.* **39**, 973, 2007; Popławski 2012; Simpson & Visser 2019), and
3. **Open-system horizon thermodynamics** (Prigogine et al., *Gen. Relativ. Gravit.* **21**, 767, 1989; Lima 1996; Jacobson 1995).

This manuscript investigates whether a single covariant variational principle across a regular two-sheeted bounce throat $\Sigma_0$ connects these three mechanisms quantitatively. Specifically, we establish three main mathematical results:

1. **Analytic orientation-reversing bounce geometry (Section 2, Propositions 1–2):** Extending the radial coordinate to $R \in (-\infty,\infty)$ with areal radius $\mathcal{R}(R) = \sqrt{R^2 + R_{\mathrm{core}}^2}$ ($R_{\mathrm{core}} = \sqrt{2\pi/\kappa} > 0$) and hybrid radial factor $M(R) = (1 + R^2/3)^{-1}$ removes the velocity cusp of unsigned $r \ge 0$ charts, reverses spatial orientation across a single throat passage ($\det F = (R/R_0)^3$, restored across two passages via $\mathcal{P}^2 = +I_3$), and yields real-analytic, uniformly bounded curvature invariants ($\mathcal{K}_{\mathrm{static}}(0) = 12/R_{\mathrm{core}}^4 < \infty$) satisfying the contracted Bianchi identity identically across $\Sigma_0$.
2. **Exact parameter-free cubic budget surface and open-horizon equations of state (Section 3, Propositions 3–4 & Theorem 5):** Reducing a covariant 3+1D scalar-gravity action onto quantized vortex-phase bundles in a parent vacuum density $P \equiv V(s_\star)$ yields a five-variable variational functional whose closed-form global minimum eliminates both internal variables $(\theta^\star, \chi)$ and confines the dimensionless vacuum-stretch ($D = \Omega_{\mathrm{DE}}$), boundary-dust ($M = \Omega_{\mathrm{DM}}$), and visible-filament ($V = \Omega_{\mathrm{vis}}$) shares to the exact cubic surface $9DVB = 4(V + B/2)^3$ ($B = M - D/3$). Natural boundary variation on an unpinned throat enforces winding chemical-potential equilibrium ($\mu_+ = \mu_-$), yielding $w_{\mathrm{DE}} = -1$ ($\rho_{\mathrm{DE}} = 3P = \mathrm{const}$) and $w_{\mathrm{DM}} = 0$.
3. **Harmonic neck cubic, three-axis freeze-out, and falsifiable cosmological readouts (Sections 4–5, Theorems 6–11 & Proposition 12):** Removing matching-sphere cutoffs in the exterior $\ell=2$ harmonic two-center neck problem yields the exact characteristic cubic $p^3 - 3p - 3 = 0$ ($p_+ = \varphi^{2/3} + \varphi^{-2/3} \approx 2.103803$). Harmonicity ($\Delta\Phi = 0$) drives a sequential three-axis orthogonal cascade terminating at $\chi_{\mathrm{freeze}} = 3$ ($\Omega_b = e^{-3} \approx 0.0498$, $\Omega_{\mathrm{DM}} = 0.2388$, $\Omega_{\mathrm{DE}} = 0.7114$).
